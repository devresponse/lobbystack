# RealInfoBot features agents use, and why they matter

What a real estate agent gets in the RealInfoBot app, feature by feature: the inbox, alerts, contacts, teaching the assistant, rules, the Questions list, signs and QR codes, scan counts, team access, calendar and CRM connections, and reusing a sign. Each feature says what it is, why it matters and how to put it simply.

## Where does the agent see each buyer inquiry?

In the RealInfoBot app at https://ca.realinfobot.com, which works in any browser, on a computer or a phone:

- **Calls** lists every call with its summary, transcript and recording.
- **Messages** is the inbox for website chats, texts to the RealInfoBot number, and messages the assistant took on calls, such as a callback or a showing request.
- **Home** shows what needs a person under Action required.
- **Contacts** builds itself from the people who leave their details.

Each inquiry shows the buyer, a summary such as "asked about the price and a Saturday showing", the full transcript and the listing it was about.

Say it simply: "Every chat and call lands in one inbox, with a summary, so you know what they asked before you call back."

Also asked as: inbox, dashboard, where are my leads, inquiries, call log, transcripts, recordings, summaries, mobile app.

## How does the agent find out a buyer asked something?

By alerts the agent chooses: by email, by text to their mobile, or both. Each person on the team picks their own, such as a text when the assistant takes a message on a call or when a buyer texts the number, and an email when a new website chat starts. Owners and admins can also let the team get an email after every call, with the summary or the whole conversation.

Example: on showing days, the agent turns on text alerts, so a showing request taken at 2 pm reaches them between appointments. On quiet days, email is enough.

Also asked as: alerts, notifications, text me, email me, lead alerts, instant notification, get notified, push notifications, how fast will I know when a buyer asks about my listing, right away.

## How does the agent teach the assistant about a listing?

By giving it the facts, in about ten minutes, in any of these ways:

- **Upload documents** the agent already has, such as the feature sheet, the listing description or a condo or strata summary: PDF, Word, text or Markdown files, up to 25 MB each.
- **Paste or type text entries,** such as "Open house: Saturday 2 to 4 pm" or the property taxes.
- **Import pages from a website address,** such as the listing's page on the agent's own site.

A text entry reaches the assistant within moments, so a new open house time or a price change is live from the next call or chat. There's no connection to MLS boards or listing portals.

Say it simply: "Your feature sheet already has the answers. Upload it, and that work does double duty."

Also asked as: setup, add listing facts, give it my listing information, listing info, upload feature sheet, training, teach the bot, knowledge base, MLS import, listing details.

## Can the agent control how the assistant handles buyers?

Yes, with rules written in plain words, the way the agent would brief a new assistant, one situation per rule. For example:

- "Before passing on a showing request, ask for the buyer's name and best number."
- "If someone asks about offers or price negotiation, say the agent will call them back."
- "If someone asks about the open house, mention it's this Saturday from 2 to 4."

Rules apply to every listing and every language, and work inside four promises the assistant keeps in every conversation: it says it's an AI, it answers only from the agent's facts, it gives no opinions, and it passes anyone who wants a person to the agent.

Why it matters: the agent's name is on the listing, and the agent decides how buyers are handled.

Also asked as: rules, control, customize, instructions, guardrails, what it says, brand, script, scripted answers.

## What is the Questions list, and why does it matter?

When a buyer asks something the listing's facts don't cover, the assistant says it doesn't have that detail, offers to take a message, and adds the question to the Questions list, with how many times buyers asked it, most asked first. The agent answers it once there, and the next buyer gets the answer within moments.

Why it matters: the assistant gets more complete every week without extra work. Clearing the list once a week covers most common questions after a week or two, and each answer saves a callback.

Say it simply: "Answer a question once, and every buyer after gets the answer."

Also asked as: unanswered questions, missed questions, improve answers, learn, gets smarter, train it over time, gaps.

## How does the agent get signs and QR codes?

The RealInfoBot app makes print-ready signs with the listing's QR code: three designs (yellow, white and a black one for dim places), a message of the agent's choosing, sizes from a full page down to cards eight to a sheet, and English, French, Spanish or Serbian. The agent can also download just the code, as a PNG or an SVG that stays sharp at any size, for a sign rider, a sticker, the feature sheet or a social post.

RealInfoBot doesn't print or ship anything: the agent prints the code or gives the file to their usual sign company.

Example: the agent orders a sign rider with the code and "Scan to ask about this home" from their sign company, and adds the code to the feature sheet.

Also asked as: print a sign, sign rider, sticker, QR code download, sign files, print shop, signage, flyer, feature sheet.

## Can the agent see which sign buyers use?

Yes. The agent makes a separate QR link for each place the code goes, named after it, such as "Yard sign", "Feature sheet" or "Open house door". Each link has its own code, and RealInfoBot counts its scans per day, kept for 13 months, so the agent sees which placements buyers actually use.

Why it matters: the agent learns where to put effort, and can tell a seller how many people scanned their sign this week. A jump in scans after an open house or a price change is worth noticing.

Also asked as: scan counts, tracking, which sign works, QR analytics, marketing results, measure, statistics, how many scans.

## Can the agent's team use RealInfoBot too?

Yes. The owner invites people by email, each with their own sign-in and a role: an admin can change settings and billing and invite others, and a viewer can see calls, contacts and activity without changing anything. Plans don't charge per team member.

Everyone sees the same inbox, calls and contacts, so an assistant or partner can follow up without being forwarded anything, and each person picks their own alerts. Removing someone takes their access away at once.

Say it simply: "No more forwarded voicemails or screenshots of texts. Your whole team works from one inbox."

Also asked as: team, partner, assistant, co-listing agent, users, seats, per-user pricing, roles, admin, viewer, shared inbox.

## Does RealInfoBot work with my calendar and my CRM?

- **Google Calendar:** connect it, and the assistant doesn't book showings over the agent's busy events, and adds each showing it books. Outlook calendars aren't supported.
- **CRMs and other tools:** webhooks send events, such as a finished call or a new message, to the agent's own systems as they happen, and API keys let another tool or a script work with the account. There's no built-in connection to a particular CRM, MLS board or listing portal.

Why it matters: the agent's calendar stays the one source of truth, and inquiries can flow into the tools the agent already uses.

Also asked as: Google Calendar, Outlook, CRM, Follow Up Boss, kvCORE, Lofty, integrations, API, webhooks, Zapier, MLS, sync.

## Can I reuse my sign or QR code for my next listing?

Yes. A QR link in RealInfoBot can't be deleted, so a printed code keeps working, and the agent can point it at another listing's assistant at any time. A sign rider or a permanent sign moves from home to home, and its code opens the new listing.

When a home sells, the agent pauses or archives its assistant, and adds the next listing in its place for as long as the pack lasts. Its calls, chats and contacts are kept.

Say it simply: "Print the rider once. When the home sells, point the code at your next listing."

Also asked as: reuse sign, next listing, permanent sign, move the rider, change listing, sold, recycle QR code.
