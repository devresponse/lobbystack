## What does the 1-listing pack include?

**Try it: 1 listing**, CA$99 paid once, gives one listing its assistant for 180 days from the payment:

- 250 voice minutes for phone calls and browser calls; at 3 minutes a call, that's about 80 calls;
- 300 website chats, each counted once however many messages it has;
- 1 RealInfoBot phone number that takes calls and texts;
- 120 call transfers to the agent's phone and 300 alert texts to the agent and their team;
- 20 MB of listing documents;
- the listing's page, QR codes and print-ready signs, and every inquiry with its summary and transcript.

Over its 180 days, CA$99 works out to under CA$17 a month, with nothing to cancel. If the home sells early, the agent can add their next listing in its place for the rest of the 180 days.

## How do the 3-listing and 6-listing packs work?

**3 listings**, CA$249, and **Pro: 6 listings**, CA$449, each last 365 days from the payment. They're everything in a single listing, times three or times six:

- up to 3 or 6 listings answering at the same time, each with its own assistant, page and QR codes;
- 750 or 1,500 voice minutes, and 1,800 or 3,600 website chats;
- 720 or 1,440 call transfers, and 1,800 or 3,600 alert texts;
- 60 MB or 120 MB of listing documents;
- 1 phone number for the account.

The minutes, chats, texts and transfers are shared by all the pack's listings, not split per listing. The listings count how many can answer at once, not credits used up: when a home sells, the agent pauses its assistant and adds the next listing in its place, as often as needed for 365 days.

## Is the 3-listing or 6-listing pack better value?

Yes, per listing. Three single listings would cost CA$297, so the 3-listing pack's CA$249 saves CA$48 and works out to CA$83 a listing. Six single listings would cost CA$594, so the 6-listing pack's CA$449 saves CA$145 and works out to CA$75 a listing. Each also lasts 365 days instead of 180.

Because a listing that sells can be replaced by the next one, an agent who swaps in new listings during the year spreads the price over even more homes, as long as the shared minutes and chats last, and a top-up adds minutes if needed.

Best fit: the 3-listing pack for an agent with a few listings a year; the 6-listing pack for an agent who always has a sign up.

## Why does RealInfoBot charge per listing instead of a monthly subscription?

Because an agent's income comes listing by listing, and the cost should follow it:

- **Costs follow the business.** A subscription bills in slow months as well as busy ones. Paying per listing means spending only when there's a home to sell.
- **Easy to justify.** One listing, one price, simple to weigh against one extra buyer or showing.
- **Nothing to remember to cancel.** When the pack ends, so does the cost. No renewals, no overage bills.

## What if RealInfoBot isn't right for me after I buy?

RealInfoBot's Terms of Service give a full refund on a listing pack if asked within 30 days of purchase, no questions asked, whatever was used. So an agent can try it on a real listing without risk. After 30 days, there's no refund, including for unused minutes or a listing that sells early.

To ask for a refund, email support@realinfobot.com with the account email and order number.

Say it simply: "Put it on a real listing. If it's not for you, ask within 30 days for a full refund."

## What if my listing gets more calls than the pack includes?

The agent adds a top-up: 100 extra voice minutes for CA$39, paid once, lasting 180 days, without changing the pack. Top-ups are sold on the free plan and on listing packs, and never renew or add themselves. An agent can also buy another pack while one is running: their minutes, chats, texts and transfers add up.

The app's Usage screen shows what's used and what's left, and owners get an email when a fifth of a pack is left. Calls shorter than 10 seconds and spam calls aren't counted.

Also asked as: run out of minutes, extra minutes, top up, busy listing, overage, more calls, add minutes, limits.

## What happens when a RealInfoBot pack ends?

Nothing renews and nothing is charged. The business goes back to the free plan: the phone number stops answering, and is held for 30 days in case the agent buys another pack; listings beyond the first are paused, not deleted; and the free plan's monthly allowances apply. The listing page and QR codes keep working within the free plan.

To keep a listing answering calls, the agent buys another pack before the end date. Owners get an email 30 days and 7 days before.

## Are RealInfoBot's prices in Canadian or US dollars?

The app charges in Canadian dollars (CAD) for agents in Canada and the United States alike.
