# Concerns agents raise about RealInfoBot, and honest answers

The doubts real estate agents most often have before trying RealInfoBot, each with an honest answer from what the app actually does, and a next step. Acknowledge the concern first; never argue or pressure.

## "I already answer my own phone."

Most agents do, when they can. RealInfoBot covers the times they can't: during a showing, on the road, with a seller, in the evening and on weekends. It doesn't replace the agent: buyers who want a person can leave their name and number, and on phone calls the assistant can put them through to the agent's own phone.

The routine questions, such as the price, the taxes or the open house time, get answered either way, and the agent calls back the buyers worth calling, knowing what each one asked.

Good question to ask: "How many calls from your signs come in while you're with a client or after 7 pm?"

Next step: suggest trying the sample listing on https://realinfobot.com/ca to hear how it handles a buyer.

Also asked as: I answer my own calls, I don't miss calls, I don't need it, my phone is always on, I pick up every call.

## "Buyers don't want to talk to a robot."

Many buyers would rather not talk to anyone yet: scanning a code and typing a question feels easier than calling an agent at 9 pm. For those who call, the assistant is upfront that it's an AI, answers right away instead of sending them to voicemail, talks at a natural pace and lets them interrupt.

Anyone who wants a person can say so, and the assistant takes their details for the agent, or puts a phone call through to the agent's number when the agent allows it.

Say it simply: "An honest AI that answers at 9 pm beats a voicemail box nobody leaves a message on."

Next step: invite them to hear it on the sample listing at https://realinfobot.com/ca.

Also asked as: people hate bots, robot voice, sounds fake, buyers want a human, AI is annoying, will people talk to it, does it sound natural.

## "What if the AI says something wrong about my listing?"

RealInfoBot answers only from the facts the agent gives it, never from general knowledge or a guess. When it doesn't know something, it says so, offers to take a message, and puts the question on the agent's Questions list. It gives no opinions on price, offers, the neighbourhood or the market.

The agent can read every conversation. If an answer was off, the agent fixes the fact or adds a rule in a minute, and the assistant uses it from the next call or chat. AI can still make mistakes, which is why every transcript is there to check.

Say it simply: "It only knows what you told it, and you can read every word it says."

Also asked as: accuracy, hallucinations, wrong information, liability, misinformation, mistakes, can I trust the answers, quality control.

## "It's too expensive" or "I can't afford another tool."

The 1-listing pack is CA$99, paid once, for 180 days, which works out to under CA$17 a month, with no subscription and nothing to cancel. On the 3-listing and 6-listing packs it's CA$83 or CA$75 a listing, for 365 days. Plans don't charge per team member.

The cost only comes when there's a listing, and it's easy to weigh against one buyer who would otherwise have driven away. There's a full refund if asked within 30 days, and the free plan costs nothing for trying it out.

Good question to ask: "What's one extra buyer conversation from your sign worth to you?"

Also asked as: too expensive, can't afford it, budget, cost too much, price objection, cheaper option, expensive, not worth it.

## "I don't have time to set it up."

Setup takes about ten minutes: name the business, upload the feature sheet or paste the listing's facts, choose a greeting and, with a pack, pick a phone number. Then print the QR code. The agent's feature sheet and listing description already hold most answers, so that work does double duty.

Rather not do it at all? RealInfoBot's team can set it up with the agent: email the listing link and the best way to reach the agent to support@realinfobot.com.

Say it simply: "If you can send an email, you can set this up. Or send us the listing link and we'll help."

Also asked as: no time, too busy, setup takes too long, complicated, hassle, learning curve, done for you, set it up for me.

## "I'm not technical."

No technical skills needed. RealInfoBot works in a web browser, on a computer or a phone, with no app to install. Setup is typing or uploading what the agent already knows, and the QR code and signs are ready to print. Buyers don't need an app either: they point their phone camera at the code or call the number.

Adding the chat to the agent's own website is one line of code, which they can send to whoever manages their site. RealInfoBot's team helps at support@realinfobot.com.

Also asked as: not tech-savvy, not good with computers, too complicated, easy to use, do I need a developer, user friendly.

## "I already have an assistant or an answering service."

RealInfoBot works alongside them. It answers buyers from the listing's facts at any hour, including nights, weekends and holidays, in the buyer's language, for one fixed price per listing, and every conversation lands in an inbox the whole team shares. A person can then spend their time on the serious buyers and the judgment calls.

An assistant can join the agent's RealInfoBot account with their own login to read every inquiry and, as an admin, reply to texts and step into chats.

Also asked as: I have an assistant, answering service, call center, receptionist, virtual assistant, ISA, my brokerage answers calls.

## "Will it talk about price or give buyers advice?"

No. The assistant shares only the facts the agent entered, such as the asking price, and gives no opinions: nothing on negotiating, offers, what the sellers would take, the neighbourhood or the market, and no legal, financial, mortgage or inspection advice. Those questions come to the agent, with the buyer's name and number.

Why it matters: the agent stays in control of every conversation that affects the seller's position, and serious buyers become callbacks.

Also asked as: negotiation, negotiate price with buyers, would they take less, advice, opinions, market questions, what's it worth, offers, representation.

## "What about privacy, and my sellers' information?"

The assistant shares only what the agent puts in its facts. It doesn't look anything up about a seller or a buyer. So agents keep private details out: lockbox and alarm codes, when the sellers are away, why they're selling, and private notes.

Buyers' details belong to the agent. RealInfoBot doesn't sell them, share them or use them for anything else, and doesn't use personal information to train AI. Every call says it's an AI and that it's recorded and transcribed. RealInfoBot is a Canadian company and follows Canadian privacy laws, including PIPEDA. The Privacy Policy is at https://realinfobot.com/ca/legal/privacy.

Also asked as: privacy, data security, seller confidentiality, recording consent, is my data safe, who sees the data, PIPEDA, GDPR.

## "Does it connect to MLS or my CRM?"

There's no direct connection to MLS boards, listing portals or a particular CRM. The agent gives the assistant its facts by uploading the feature sheet or other documents, pasting text, or importing pages from a website address, such as the listing's page on the agent's own site. That takes minutes, and the agent controls exactly what the assistant knows.

To send inquiries to a CRM, RealInfoBot has webhooks, which send each finished call or new message to the agent's own systems, and API keys. Google Calendar connects for showings.

Also asked as: MLS integration, pull my listings from MLS, REALTOR.ca, Zillow, IDX, CRM integration, Follow Up Boss, kvCORE, automatic import, sync listings.

## "My listing will sell quickly. Is it worth it for a few weeks?"

Yes, because nothing is lost when a home sells. The agent pauses the sold home's assistant and adds the next listing in its place for as long as the pack lasts: 180 days for the 1-listing pack, 365 days for the 3-listing and 6-listing packs. A sign rider's QR code can point to the new listing, and the buyers who asked about the sold home stay in the agent's contacts for the next one.

If there's no next listing, the pack simply ends. There's nothing to cancel.

Also asked as: fast market, sells in a week, short listing, hot market, quick sale, waste of money, unused days.

## "I want buyers to call my own number."

The assistant answers the RealInfoBot number that comes with a listing pack, not the agent's existing mobile or office number. That keeps the agent's personal number off the sign, and when a buyer on a phone call asks for the agent, the assistant can put them through to the agent's own number.

Buyers who text the RealInfoBot number reach the agent's inbox, and the agent replies from the app, from the same RealInfoBot number.

Also asked as: keep my number, use my existing number, forward my number, port my number, my cell on the sign, call forwarding.
