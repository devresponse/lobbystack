# Trusting RealInfoBot: the AI's promises, recordings, privacy and data

What a real estate agent should know before handing their sign to an AI: the promises the assistant keeps, how callers are told about the AI and the recording, who owns the conversations and contacts, which AI it uses, where data is processed, how long it's kept, and what to keep out of the assistant's facts. The full Privacy Policy is at https://realinfobot.com/ca/legal/privacy.

## What promises does RealInfoBot's assistant keep?

Four, in every conversation and every language:

1. **It says it's an AI.** It never pretends to be a person, and it never calls or texts anyone first.
2. **It only knows what the agent told it.** It answers from the agent's facts and settings, never from general knowledge or a guess. If it doesn't know, it says so and takes a message.
3. **It gives no opinions.** No comments on price, offers, the neighbourhood or the market, and no legal, financial, mortgage or inspection advice. Those questions come to the agent.
4. **It passes people who want a person to the agent.** It takes their name and number, or puts a phone call through to the agent's number if the agent allows it.

The agent's own rules work inside these promises.

Say it simply: "Handing your sign to an AI is a big step. It says it's an AI, only knows what you told it, never gives opinions, and you can read every conversation."

Also asked as: guardrails, AI safety, is it safe, will it embarrass me, rules it follows, what it won't say, control, brand safety.

## Do callers know they're talking to an AI, and are calls recorded?

Yes. Right after the agent's greeting, every call says the caller is speaking with an AI assistant and that the call is recorded and transcribed, in the language the call starts in. A call from the listing page says the same, and in a chat the assistant never pretends to be a person.

Every call is recorded and transcribed, so the agent can play it, read it and download it from the app. Many places require telling callers that a call is recorded, and RealInfoBot says it on every call by default.

Also asked as: AI disclosure, recording notice, consent, is this call recorded, transparency, do people know it's AI, legal to record.

## Who owns the conversations and contacts?

The agent's business does. The calls, chats, messages and contacts a listing's assistant collects belong to the agent's business. RealInfoBot uses them only to provide the service, and for security, billing and the law. It doesn't sell them, share them for advertising or use them for its own marketing.

For the buyers who scan the sign or call the number, the agent decides how their information is used, and RealInfoBot processes it on the agent's behalf as a service provider.

Say it simply: "Your callers, your listing, your contacts. We don't sell them, share them or use them for anything else."

Also asked as: data ownership, my leads, do you sell data, who owns the contacts, lead ownership, sharing data.

## Which AI does RealInfoBot use, and is my data used to train it?

Conversations are handled by OpenAI models. RealInfoBot sends AI providers only what the task needs, such as the conversation and the listing's facts. RealInfoBot doesn't use personal information to train AI models, and its providers' business terms don't allow them to train on it.

If the agent connects Google Calendar, event titles and descriptions are never sent to AI providers.

Also asked as: OpenAI, ChatGPT, GPT, which AI, AI model, AI training, is my data used for training, LLM.

## Where is RealInfoBot's data processed and how is it protected?

RealInfoBot is a Canadian company. It and its providers process information in Canada, the United States and other countries where the providers operate, such as OpenAI for the AI, Twilio for phone numbers and texts, and Polar for payments. The full list is at https://realinfobot.com/ca/legal/subprocessors.

Safeguards include encryption in transit, separation of each customer's data, restricted staff access and logging. Each person on a team has their own sign-in with a role. RealInfoBot follows PIPEDA, Alberta's PIPA and other Canadian privacy laws, including Quebec's Law 25.

Also asked as: data residency, where is data stored, Canada, security, encryption, PIPEDA, Law 25, GDPR, subprocessors, compliance.

## How long does RealInfoBot keep recordings and messages?

On a listing pack, call recordings and transcripts are kept for 90 days, and buyer inquiries and messages for 365 days. On the free plan, they're kept for 30 days. After that, a call stays in the list without them. An agent who wants to keep a recording longer downloads it from the app.

An owner can delete the whole business at any time, which erases its calls, recordings, chats, contacts and listing facts for good.

Also asked as: data retention, how long are recordings kept, delete my data, history, archive, keep transcripts.

## What should agents keep out of the assistant's facts?

The assistant shares what the agent puts in its facts, and doesn't look anything up about a seller or a buyer. So agents keep private details out:

- lockbox and alarm codes;
- when the sellers are away, or their schedule;
- why they're selling, their bottom line, or anything told in confidence;
- private notes about a buyer or a seller.

Getting the seller's OK before their listing details go in is good practice.

Why it matters: what's in the facts is what buyers can hear, so the agent stays in control of the seller's privacy.

Also asked as: seller privacy, confidential information, lockbox code, safety, sensitive information, what not to share, security risk.

## Is RealInfoBot an emergency service, and what if it's down?

RealInfoBot isn't an emergency service, and the assistant isn't a way to reach emergency help: a caller with an emergency should call 911 or local emergency services.

Like any online service, RealInfoBot can be interrupted by maintenance or by outages at carriers, AI providers or hosting providers. Every conversation it does handle is in the agent's inbox, and the agent can always reach buyers who left their details.

Also asked as: emergencies, 911, uptime, reliability, outage, downtime, what if it stops working, availability.
