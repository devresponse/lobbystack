# Why RealInfoBot: what it is, the problem it solves and who it's for

RealInfoBot turns a real estate listing's sign into an assistant that answers buyers' questions about the home, day or night, in the buyer's language, and sends the agent every inquiry. This document gives the short pitch, the problem it solves, who it's for and what makes it different.

## What is RealInfoBot, in one sentence?

RealInfoBot gives each real estate listing its own AI assistant: buyers scan the QR code on the sign, open the listing's web page or, with a listing pack, call the listing's RealInfoBot number, and the assistant answers their questions about the home, day or night, in their language. Every inquiry comes to the agent with a summary and the full transcript.

The assistant answers only from the facts the agent gives it, says it's an AI, and passes anything it can't answer, and anyone who wants a person, to the agent.

Say it simply: "One sign. One property. One voice. Your sign answers questions about the home, day or night, and sends you every inquiry."

## What problem does RealInfoBot solve for real estate agents?

People already stop at a listing's sign, and most of them drive away. A couple slows down at 8 pm. They like the house and want the price. They won't call an agent that late, and they won't leave a voicemail. By morning they've moved on, and the agent never knew they existed.

RealInfoBot gives that sign a way to answer. The buyer scans the code or calls, gets answers about the home right away, and the agent gets the inquiry with a summary and the transcript, ready for a follow-up call.

The same gap opens whenever the agent can't pick up: at a showing, driving, sitting with a seller, at dinner or asleep.

Good question to ask: "When a buyer calls the number on your sign at 8 pm, what happens today?"

## Who is RealInfoBot for?

RealInfoBot is for real estate professionals who put up signs and advertise properties: solo agents, teams and brokerages, and landlords and property managers with a home for sale, for lease or for rent. It's a business service for people 18 or older.

It suits most:

- solo agents at showings all day, who can't answer every call from the sign;
- teams that want one shared inbox for several listings;
- agents whose buyers speak many languages;
- anyone who wants evening and weekend drive-bys to get answers instead of a voicemail.

## What makes RealInfoBot different from other AI assistants?

- **Built around one listing.** Each listing gets its own AI assistant, web page and QR codes, so a buyer who scans a sign asks about that home without saying which one.
- **Only the agent's facts.** It never answers from general knowledge or a guess, and it says when it doesn't know.
- **The buyer's language.** It answers in the language the buyer speaks or writes, with nothing to switch on.
- **Every inquiry to the agent,** with a summary and the full transcript. The contacts belong to the agent, and RealInfoBot doesn't sell or share them.
- **Paid once per listing,** with no subscription and nothing that renews.
- **Ready in about ten minutes,** or RealInfoBot's team sets it up with you.

Say it simply: "It isn't a general chatbot. It's your listing's own assistant, and it only says what you told it."

## How does RealInfoBot help an agent with sellers?

Sellers want every interested buyer heard. With RealInfoBot, the seller's sign answers buyers day and night, in the buyer's language, from facts the agent approved, and the agent sees every question buyers asked.

That gives an agent something concrete to show in a listing presentation: scan the sign together, let the seller ask it a question, and explain that every inquiry about their home comes to the agent with a transcript. During the listing, the agent can tell the seller what buyers ask about most, from the Questions list and the conversations, and how many people scanned each sign, from the scan counts.

Keep the seller's private details out of the assistant's facts: lockbox codes, when the home is empty, and why they're selling.

Example: "Your sign will answer buyers at 9 pm in Punjabi or Spanish, and I'll see every question in the morning."
