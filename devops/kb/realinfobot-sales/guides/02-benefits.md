# What RealInfoBot does for an agent: the benefits

The reasons real estate agents use RealInfoBot, each with why it matters and an example: catching the buyers who drive away, saving time, faster follow-up, every language, free evenings, sellers, contacts and knowing what works.

## How does RealInfoBot help me catch the buyers who would drive away?

Most people who slow down at a sign never call. It's late, they aren't sure they're serious yet, or they don't want a sales conversation. Scanning a QR code feels like less of a commitment than calling an agent: there's no one to talk to and no wait for a callback. So people who would never call can still ask their question, and every question is a chance for the agent to hear about them.

With RealInfoBot, those buyers get the answer on the spot, from the agent's own facts, and each conversation that leaves a name or number lands in the agent's inbox with the transcript.

Example: a couple scans the sign at 8:15 pm, asks the price and whether the basement is finished, and leaves a number for a Saturday showing. The agent reads it at breakfast and calls them first.

Also asked as: more leads, more buyers, capture leads, lead generation, stop losing buyers, catch drive-bys, convert sign traffic.

## Will RealInfoBot save me time?

Yes, on the questions agents answer over and over: "Is it still available?", "How much is it?", "How many bedrooms?", "What are the taxes?", "When is the open house?" The assistant answers them from the listing's facts, so the agent's phone isn't buzzing for answers a buyer can get in ten seconds.

The agent spends the time saved on the buyers who matter. Each inquiry's summary shows who was just curious and who asked to book a showing, so the agent knows who to call back first. When the assistant can't answer something, the question goes on the Questions list, and the agent answers it once for every buyer after.

Say it simply: "It handles the same five questions every buyer asks, so you only take the calls that move a sale forward."

Also asked as: save time, too many calls, repetitive questions, busy agent, time management, fewer interruptions, efficiency.

## How does RealInfoBot help me follow up with buyers?

Every conversation reaches the agent ready for a follow-up call: who it was, a one-line summary such as "asked about the price and a Saturday showing", the full transcript, a recording for calls, and which listing it was about. Alerts by email, by text or both say when something comes in, so a message taken during a showing reaches the agent between appointments.

The Home screen lists what needs a person, such as a callback or a showing request, and the people who leave their details become contacts with their whole history.

Example: instead of a voicemail that says "call me back" with half a number, the agent gets "Sarah, asked if the double garage fits a truck and wants to see it Saturday morning", with her number.

Also asked as: follow up, callback, lead alerts, notifications, respond faster, speed to lead, inbox, lead details.

## How does RealInfoBot help with buyers who speak other languages?

RealInfoBot's assistant answers in the language the buyer speaks or writes: English, French, Spanish, Chinese, Punjabi, Hindi, Korean, Ukrainian and many others, with nothing to switch on. A call opens in the language the agent chooses and switches when a caller speaks another one. The agent writes the listing's facts once, in their own language, and the assistant never adds anything the agent didn't give it.

Each call's summary is written in the business's language, even when the buyer spoke another one, and the transcript shows exactly what was said.

Why it matters: the buyer for a home may be more comfortable in another language, or shopping for a parent who is. People ask fuller questions in their first language, and nobody walks away because they couldn't ask.

Also asked as: multilingual, languages, French, Chinese, Mandarin, Cantonese, Punjabi, Hindi, Spanish, Korean, Ukrainian, newcomers, translate, ESL buyers.

## Can RealInfoBot answer buyers at night and on weekends?

Yes. The assistant answers calls and chats 24 hours a day, every day, including holidays, while the agent keeps their own hours. Buyers often look at homes in the evening and on weekends, exactly when an agent stops picking up, and those inquiries wait for the agent in the morning with their summaries.

The assistant runs in the cloud, so it keeps answering when the agent's phone is off.

Say it simply: "You keep your hours. Your sign keeps answering."

Also asked as: after hours, nights, weekends, evenings, holidays, 24/7, always on, when I'm asleep, vacation, time off, work-life balance.

## Does RealInfoBot keep my personal phone number off the sign?

Yes, with a listing pack. The sign shows the QR code and the account's RealInfoBot number, and the assistant answers that number. The agent's own mobile number stays private.

When a buyer on a phone call asks for the agent, the assistant can put the call through to the agent's own phone, if the agent sets a transfer number. When the agent replies to a buyer's text from the RealInfoBot app, the reply goes out from the RealInfoBot number too.

Also asked as: privacy, keep my cell number private, personal number, don't want my number on the sign, spam calls, separate business line.

## Do I keep the buyers' contact details?

Yes. Everyone who calls, texts or books, and every chat visitor who leaves an email address or phone number, becomes a contact in the agent's RealInfoBot account, with their calls, messages and transcripts. The agent doesn't type anything in.

The contacts belong to the agent. RealInfoBot doesn't sell them, share them or use them for anything else. A buyer who wasn't right for one home might be right for the agent's next listing.

Say it simply: "Your callers, your listing, your contacts."

Also asked as: who owns the leads, my contacts, lead database, do you sell my leads, CRM, buyer list, keep my leads.

## How does RealInfoBot show me what's working?

- **Scan counts for each sign.** Make a separate QR link for each place the code goes, such as the yard sign, the feature sheet and the open house door, and RealInfoBot counts each one's scans per day, kept for 13 months.
- **The Questions list** shows what buyers asked that the facts didn't cover, most asked first.
- **Analytics** charts calls, messages and bookings over time and how buyers reach the agent.
- **Transcripts** show exactly what each buyer wanted.

Example: five people asked about parking this week, so the agent adds the parking details and mentions them at the next open house.

Also asked as: reports, statistics, analytics, tracking, which sign works, marketing results, insights, measure, ROI.
