# RealInfoBot features buyers use, and how to explain them

What a home buyer can do with a RealInfoBot listing, feature by feature: scan the sign, call, text, ask in any language, book a showing, reach the agent, and chat on the agent's website or listing page. Each feature says what it is, why it matters and how to put it simply.

## How does a buyer ask a question by scanning the sign?

Every RealInfoBot listing has its own web page and QR code. A buyer points their phone camera at the code on the sign, and the listing's page opens with a chat. They type a question, such as "How much is it?" or "Is there a basement?", and the assistant answers from the agent's facts. There's no app to download.

With a listing pack, the buyer can also tap to call the assistant from the page and ask out loud, with no phone line.

Why it matters: a scan feels like less of a commitment than a phone call, so curious buyers ask their question instead of driving away. Because the code belongs to one listing, nobody has to say which home they mean.

Say it simply: "Buyers scan your sign, and a chat about that home opens on their phone."

Also asked as: QR code, scan the sign, scan to chat, how do buyers use it, do buyers need an app, phone camera, sign rider, chat on the sign.

## Can buyers call my listing and talk to the assistant?

Yes, with a listing pack, which includes one RealInfoBot phone number for the agent's account. The assistant answers calls to that number out loud, any hour, from the listing's facts, and switches to the caller's language when they speak another one. Each call can last up to 30 minutes, and several buyers can call at the same time: each call is its own conversation, so nobody waits for another buyer to finish.

Buyers can also call from the listing's page in their web browser, when the business has a phone number and voice minutes left.

With several listings, the agent's main AI agent answers the one number from every listing's facts, and callers say which home they mean. The free plan has no phone number.

Say it simply: "Some buyers would rather call than scan. The number on your sign answers out loud, day or night."

Also asked as: phone calls, call the sign, voice, talk to it, phone number, does it answer the phone, AI phone answering, call the listing.

## Can buyers text my listing?

Buyers can text the RealInfoBot number, and each text lands in the agent's Messages inbox with an alert. The agent or their team replies by hand from the app, and the reply goes out from the RealInfoBot number, so the agent's own number stays private.

The AI doesn't reply to texts: it answers by chat, browser call and phone call. Be clear about this with anyone who asks.

Why it matters: a quiet buyer can still send a quick question, and the agent answers it from the same inbox as the calls and chats.

Say it simply: "Buyers can text the number, and you reply from the app. The AI answers the chats and calls."

Also asked as: SMS, text messages, text the sign, texting, does it answer texts, auto reply, text back, text for info.

## Does the assistant answer in the buyer's language?

Yes. Buyers can ask in their own language, such as English, French, Spanish, Chinese, Punjabi, Hindi, Korean or Ukrainian, among many others, and the assistant answers in it. A call opens in the language the agent chooses and switches when the caller speaks another one, with no menu to press. A chat replies in the language the buyer writes in.

The agent writes the facts once, in their own language, and the assistant answers from them without adding anything.

Example: a buyer scans the sign and types "还有几个卧室？" ("How many bedrooms?"), and gets the answer in Chinese: four bedrooms, three bathrooms and a double garage.

Also asked as: languages, multilingual, bilingual, French, Spanish, Chinese, Mandarin, Punjabi, Hindi, Korean, Ukrainian, translation.

## What does the assistant do when it doesn't know an answer?

It says so and offers to take a message, instead of guessing. The question goes on the agent's Questions list, with how many times buyers asked it. The agent answers it once, and the next buyer gets the answer.

The assistant answers only from the facts the agent gave it, never from general knowledge. It gives no opinions on price, offers, the neighbourhood or the market, and no legal, financial, mortgage or inspection advice: those questions come to the agent.

Why it matters: the agent's name is on the listing, and the assistant never makes something up to fill a silence.

Say it simply: "If it doesn't know, it says so and takes a message. It never guesses."

Also asked as: wrong answers, hallucinate, make things up, accuracy, what if it doesn't know, guessing, reliable, mistakes.

## Can buyers book a showing through RealInfoBot?

Yes, when the agent turns booking on. On the AI settings screen, the agent chooses:

- **Books appointments:** the assistant checks the agent's open times and books the showing itself. It needs a showing service, such as a 30-minute showing, and the agent's hours. With Google Calendar connected, it doesn't book over the agent's busy events, and each booking goes on the calendar.
- **Takes requests:** the assistant notes the buyer's preferred time, and the request reaches the agent as a callback to confirm.
- **Doesn't book:** the assistant takes a message instead.

Buyers who agree on a phone call can get a text confirmation and reminder.

Say it simply: "Buyers can ask for a Saturday showing at 9 pm, and it's on your list, or your calendar, by morning."

Also asked as: book a showing, schedule a viewing, appointments, calendar booking, showing requests, Google Calendar, open house booking.

## Can a buyer reach me instead of the AI?

Yes. When a buyer wants a person, the assistant takes their name and number and tells them the agent will call. On a phone call, it can also put the caller straight through to the agent's own number, when the agent sets a transfer number.

In a chat, the agent can step in at any time from the Messages screen: writing in the chat takes it over, and the assistant stops answering there until the agent hands it back. Where the agent turns on live chat for the website widget, a visitor can ask for a person, and the team has 3 minutes to join before the assistant offers to take a message.

Why it matters: the assistant handles the routine questions, and serious buyers still reach the agent.

Also asked as: talk to a human, real person, transfer, put me through, live chat, take over the chat, speak to the agent, human handoff.

## Does each listing get a web page I can share?

Yes. Each listing's assistant has its own page on the agent's RealInfoBot business page, with a chat, and the agent's name, contact details and map if they choose. The QR codes open it, and the agent can share its link anywhere: a social post, an email signature or a text to a buyer. The page stays private until the agent publishes it.

With several listings, visitors on the business page can choose which home they're asking about.

Example: the agent adds "Questions about 42 Aspen Crescent? Ask here" with the page link to their email signature, so everyone they email during the listing can ask.

Also asked as: listing page, landing page, web page, link to share, social media, Facebook, Instagram, email signature, no website.

## Can I put the same chat on my own website?

Yes. The agent creates a website widget in the RealInfoBot app and pastes one short line of code into their website, or sends it to whoever manages the site. A chat button then appears on the agent's pages and answers from the same facts as the sign and the phone. Only the websites the agent lists can run the chat. Website chats land in the same Messages inbox, and the chat can ask visitors for their email or phone number.

If the app says new widgets aren't available yet, RealInfoBot's support team can help at support@realinfobot.com.

Say it simply: "One assistant, one set of facts: your sign, your listing page and your website all answer the same way."

Also asked as: website chat, chatbot for my website, widget, embed, WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, add to my site, live chat on website.
