# RealInfoBot for solo agents, teams, brokerages, new agents and landlords

Whether RealInfoBot fits each kind of real estate professional, what each one gets out of it and which plan usually fits, plus honest answers about when RealInfoBot isn't the right fit.

## Is RealInfoBot right for a solo agent?

Yes. A solo agent is the agent, the marketer and the receptionist at once, at showings all day, while calls from the sign come in during drives, closings and dinner. Most of those go to voicemail, and most of those buyers never leave a message.

RealInfoBot answers those buyers from the agent's facts, day or night, and the agent decides who to call back from the summaries. Evenings stay the agent's own, and their personal number stays off the sign.

A day with RealInfoBot: three overnight inquiries with summaries at 7:30 am; a buyer scans the sign during an 11 am showing and gets the bedroom count at once; a Saturday showing request comes in at 6:15 pm; a 9:40 pm buyer asks in Punjabi and leaves a name. The agent calls back the showing request first.

Best fit: the 1-listing pack for one listing, or the 3-listing pack for an agent who lists a few homes a year.

Also asked as: solo agent, independent agent, one-person business, realtor on my own, no assistant, single agent.

## Is RealInfoBot right for a real estate team?

Yes. A team gets one RealInfoBot account with a shared inbox, contacts and Questions list, and each listing gets its own assistant, page and QR codes. Each person signs in with their own login, as an admin or a viewer, and chooses their own alerts. Rules apply to every listing, so buyers get the same standard on every sign, in every language. Plans don't charge per team member.

Why it matters: no more forwarded voicemails or leads that fall between people. Whoever follows up sees the whole conversation.

Best fit: the 3-listing pack for up to 3 listings at once, or the 6-listing pack for up to 6, each for 365 days. For more, email support@realinfobot.com.

Good question to ask: "How many listings does your team usually have at the same time, and who follows up on inquiries today?"

Also asked as: team, real estate team, partners, team lead, several agents, group, co-listing, team plan.

## Is RealInfoBot right for a brokerage?

RealInfoBot works for a brokerage's listings the same way as for a team: an assistant for each listing, one inbox and team members with their own logins and roles. An account can also own up to 10 separate businesses, such as one for each team, each with its own plan, listings, team and number.

For a whole brokerage, RealInfoBot's team talks through what's needed, such as how many listings at once and who should see what. Email support@realinfobot.com, and a person answers Monday to Friday, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. Mountain time.

Next step: offer to take a message with the person's name, number, brokerage and how many agents and listings they have, so RealInfoBot's team can follow up, or give support@realinfobot.com.

## Is RealInfoBot right for a new agent with one listing?

Yes. A new agent can start with one listing and pay only for that one: the 1-listing pack is CA$99, paid once, for 180 days, with no subscription. Setup takes about ten minutes, or RealInfoBot's team can set it up with them.

It also gives a new agent something to show sellers: a sign that answers buyers day and night, in their language, with every inquiry reaching the agent.

To try it first, the free plan sets up one listing with its page and QR code, with no payment, and the sample listing on https://realinfobot.com/ca can be scanned or called right away.

Also asked as: new agent, first listing, just licensed, one listing, starting out, rookie, small budget, getting started in real estate.

## Is RealInfoBot right for landlords and property managers?

Yes, for a property for rent or for lease. Renters ask the same questions over and over: whether it's still available, the rent, pets, parking, utilities and when they can see it. The assistant answers them from the landlord's facts, any hour, takes the details of people who want a viewing, and can book viewings into open times when booking is turned on.

Each property gets its own assistant, page and QR code, so the "For rent" sign or the online ad leads to answers about that unit.

Best fit: the 1-listing pack for one property, or the 3-listing or 6-listing pack for several properties at once. RealInfoBot is a business service, for people advertising a property, not for people looking for a home.

Also asked as: landlord, property manager, rental, for rent, for lease, apartment, tenants, rental units, leasing agent.

## What kinds of properties does RealInfoBot work for?

Any property the agent describes. The assistant answers only from the facts the agent gives it, so it works for a house, a condo, a townhouse, an acreage, a new build or a rental, with its open house times and how to book a showing.

Condo and strata fees, what they include and the building's rules are common questions, so they're worth adding to the facts.

Also asked as: condos, townhouses, acreage, farms, luxury homes, new construction, pre-construction, commercial, vacant land, property types.

## When is RealInfoBot not the right fit?

RealInfoBot is honest about what it doesn't do, so an agent can decide quickly:

- **AI replies to texts:** buyers can text the number, but the agent replies by hand. The AI answers chats and calls.
- **Answering an existing number:** the assistant answers the RealInfoBot number in the app, not the agent's own mobile or office number.
- **Calling buyers back:** it never calls or texts anyone first.
- **Advice:** it gives no opinions on price, offers or the market.
- **An MLS feed:** listing facts come from the agent's documents, text and website pages.
- **Emergencies:** it isn't an emergency line.

If these matter, a short conversation with support@realinfobot.com is the best next step.
