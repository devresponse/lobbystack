# RealInfoBot compared with voicemail, answering services and other options

How RealInfoBot compares with what real estate agents use today: voicemail, a mobile number on the sign, a human answering service, hiring an assistant, a general website chatbot, and a sign with a text-for-info code. Each comparison uses RealInfoBot's own facts and is fair about what the other option does well. Never name or criticize another company.

## How is RealInfoBot different from voicemail?

Voicemail asks the buyer to wait, and many buyers don't leave a message for an agent they haven't worked with yet: they hang up and call the next sign, or wait until they forget.

RealInfoBot answers right away, any hour: it gives the buyer the facts they wanted, takes their name and number, and sends the agent a summary and the transcript instead of "call me back" and half a phone number.

Say it simply: "Voicemail takes a message from the few who leave one. RealInfoBot answers everyone."

Also asked as: voicemail, versus voicemail, answering machine, missed call message, call me back, compared to voicemail.

## How is RealInfoBot different from putting my mobile number on the sign?

A mobile number on the sign works when the agent can answer. It rings during showings, drives, dinners and nights, and every buyer gets the agent's personal number.

With RealInfoBot, the sign carries the listing's QR code and, with a pack, the RealInfoBot number. Buyers get answers at any hour without interrupting the agent, the agent's personal number stays private, and a buyer on a phone call can still be put through to the agent's own phone when the agent sets a transfer number.

Also asked as: my cell on the sign, phone number on sign rider, call me sign, personal number, versus my own phone.

## How is RealInfoBot different from a human answering service?

A person at an answering service is good at judgment calls and unusual situations, though they may not know the details of each listing.

RealInfoBot's assistant knows each listing's facts, answers buyers' questions about the home right away, in the buyer's language, at any hour, and handles several conversations at once. Every conversation has a full transcript the agent can read, and the price is fixed per listing: CA$99 once for 180 days, or less per listing on the 3-listing and 6-listing packs, with no per-call or monthly fees.

Some agents may want both: RealInfoBot for the listing questions, and a person for everything else.

Also asked as: answering service, call center, better than a call center, virtual receptionist, live answering, human receptionist, versus a person, outsourced reception.

## Is RealInfoBot cheaper than hiring an assistant?

RealInfoBot doesn't replace what an assistant does, but for answering buyers' listing questions it costs CA$99 per listing, once, for 180 days, or CA$75 to CA$83 a listing on the bigger packs, and it answers at night, on weekends and on holidays, in many languages, without breaks.

An agent with an assistant can give them their own login, so the assistant reads every inquiry the AI handled and spends their time on follow-ups and the serious buyers.

Also asked as: hire an assistant, cost of an assistant, ISA, inside sales agent, admin help, receptionist salary, versus hiring.

## How is RealInfoBot different from a general AI chatbot?

A general chatbot answers from what it learned elsewhere, and can sound sure about things it doesn't know. RealInfoBot's assistant is built for one listing: it answers only from the facts the agent gave it, says when it doesn't know and takes a message, gives no opinions on price or the market, and tells every caller it's an AI.

It's also built around the sign: each listing has its own QR code, page and assistant, and every conversation that leaves a name or number becomes a contact with a transcript in the agent's inbox.

## How is RealInfoBot different from a sign with a text-for-info code?

A text-for-info code often sends back a set reply, such as a link to the listing. RealInfoBot's assistant holds a conversation: the buyer asks whatever they want, in their own words and language, and gets answers from the agent's facts, by chat or by voice, day or night.

Buyers can text the RealInfoBot number too, though the AI doesn't answer texts: they land in the agent's inbox, and the agent replies from the app.

Also asked as: text for info, SMS sign rider, text code, keyword texting, info line, sign call capture, talking house.

## How is RealInfoBot different from other AI receptionists?

RealInfoBot is built for real estate listings, rather than for any kind of business:

- Each listing gets its own assistant, web page, QR codes and print-ready signs, and a QR link for each placement counts its scans.
- Buyers can ask by scanning, chatting or calling, in their own language.
- It's paid once per listing, with no subscription.
- It follows four promises: it says it's an AI, answers only from the agent's facts, gives no opinions, and passes anyone who wants a person to the agent.
