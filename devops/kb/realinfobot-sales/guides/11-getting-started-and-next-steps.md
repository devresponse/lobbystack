# Getting started with RealInfoBot: next steps

How to try RealInfoBot, sign up, get a listing answering buyers, have RealInfoBot's team set it up, and reach a person. Offer one next step at a time, matched to where the person is: curious, comparing, or ready to start.

## How do I sign up for RealInfoBot?

Go to https://ca.realinfobot.com/en/signup, the same page the website's "Add it to your listing" buttons open. Enter an email address and a password, then the 6-digit code RealInfoBot emails. Signing up is free.

Setup then walks through these steps, in about ten minutes: the business's name, such as the agent's own name or their team's; the plan, where the free plan needs no payment and a listing pack opens a secure payment page; the website's address, which can be skipped; the listing's facts, by uploading the feature sheet or pasting text; the greeting callers hear; and, with a pack, a local phone number.

Next step: share the sign-up link, https://ca.realinfobot.com/en/signup.

Also asked as: sign up, create an account, register, get started, start now, buy, purchase, join, open an account, where do I start.

## How do I try RealInfoBot before signing up?

The website, https://realinfobot.com/ca, has a sample listing, a four-bedroom family home. Anyone can chat with its assistant on the page or call it, and ask what a buyer would ask: the price, the yard, the basement, a Saturday showing, in any language. It's the quickest way to hear how RealInfoBot answers.

After signing up, the free plan lets the agent build their own listing and test it with Test Call in the dashboard.

Next step: "Try asking the sample listing a question a buyer would ask you, at https://realinfobot.com/ca."

Also asked as: demo, try it, test it, see it in action, hear it, sample listing, live demo, example, book a demo.

## Can RealInfoBot's team set it up for me?

Yes. Setup takes about ten minutes for an agent who does it themselves, and RealInfoBot's team can walk the agent through it or set it up with them, including the listing's facts, its QR code and its phone number, so the agent can try it before buyers do.

Email support@realinfobot.com with the listing link and the best way to reach the agent. A person answers Monday to Friday, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. Mountain time.

Next step: offer to take a message with the person's name, phone number and listing link, so RealInfoBot's team can follow up, or give support@realinfobot.com.

Also asked as: done for you, set it up for me, help me set up, white glove, onboarding help, setup service, I'm too busy to set it up.

## How soon can my sign start answering buyers?

The same day. Setup takes about ten minutes, and the listing's assistant answers as soon as its facts are in and its page is published with its chat. With a listing pack, the phone number is ready moments after the agent picks it.

The agent prints the QR code from the app, or downloads it for a sign rider or sticker from their usual sign company, which can take longer. In the meantime, the code works on the feature sheet, in an email signature or in a social post, and the listing's page link can be shared anywhere.

Also asked as: how long does it take, how fast, same day, when can I start, setup time, turnaround, go live.

## What should I have ready before I start?

- The listing's facts: price, size, bedrooms and bathrooms, features, taxes, condo or strata fees, parking, schools, open house times and anything buyers often ask.
- The feature sheet or listing description as a PDF or Word file, if there is one.
- The phone number calls should be put through to when a buyer asks for the agent, for transfers.
- About ten minutes.

## Can I earn money by referring other agents to RealInfoBot?

Yes. Everyone who signs in to RealInfoBot gets their own referral link in the app's affiliate program. When a business they referred pays for RealInfoBot, they earn 20% of each payment it makes in its first 12 months. Commissions are held 30 days in case of a refund, then paid monthly through PayPal once they reach the minimum payout of $100.

Agents who share their link describe RealInfoBot honestly and say they may earn a commission.

## Can I resell RealInfoBot to my clients?

Not without a written agreement with Real Info Box. The Terms of Service and Acceptable Use Policy don't allow reselling, renting or offering RealInfoBot to others without one. To talk about it, email support@realinfobot.com.
