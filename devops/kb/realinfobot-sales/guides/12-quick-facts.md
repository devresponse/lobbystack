# RealInfoBot quick facts

Short, factual answers to questions people ask before they buy RealInfoBot: apps, languages, countries, phone numbers, voices, call length, calling out, documents, listings, team members and support.

## Is there a RealInfoBot app to download?

No app to install, for the agent or for buyers. The agent uses the dashboard at https://ca.realinfobot.com in any web browser, on a computer or a phone, and gets alerts by email or text. Buyers point their phone camera at the QR code or call the number.

The dashboard is available in English, French, Spanish and Serbian.

Also asked as: mobile app, iPhone app, Android app, app store, download, install, use on my phone.

## Which languages does RealInfoBot speak?

The assistant answers buyers in the language they speak or write, including English, French, Spanish, Chinese, Punjabi, Hindi, Korean and Ukrainian, among many others. A call opens in the language the agent chooses for the listing and switches when the caller speaks another one. The agent writes the facts once, in their own language.

Also asked as: languages, French, Spanish, Chinese, Punjabi, Hindi, Korean, Ukrainian, bilingual, multilingual, how many languages.

## Which countries does RealInfoBot work in?

RealInfoBot serves real estate agents in Canada and the United States, with website pages for each: https://realinfobot.com/ca and https://realinfobot.com/usa. Everyone signs up at https://ca.realinfobot.com. Prices are in Canadian dollars for both.

Phone numbers are local numbers in the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom or Australia. The agent picks one by area code in the United States and Canada.

Also asked as: Canada, USA, United States, UK, Australia, international, countries, local number, area code.

## What kind of phone number do I get?

Every listing pack includes one local RealInfoBot phone number for the agent's account, which takes calls and texts. The agent picks it in the app, by area code in the United States and Canada. Toll-free numbers aren't offered. The free plan has no phone number.

The agent can change the number once; the old one keeps working for 30 days so callers can learn the new one.

Also asked as: phone number, local number, toll-free, 1-800 number, vanity number, area code, choose my number, how many numbers.

## Can I choose the assistant's voice or name?

The agent chooses the greeting, the first sentence callers hear, and the language calls start in. Each listing's assistant has a name the agent sets, such as the home's address. The app doesn't offer a choice of voices.

Also asked as: voice, male voice, female voice, accent, choose the voice, assistant name, customize greeting, sound.

## How long can a call last, and how many buyers can call at once?

A call can last up to 30 minutes, with a warning a minute before the limit. Several buyers can call or chat at the same time: each conversation is its own, so nobody waits for another buyer to finish. Calls shorter than 10 seconds and spam calls don't count toward the minutes.

Also asked as: call length, how long can they talk, simultaneous calls, several calls at once, busy signal, call limit.

## Does RealInfoBot call buyers back or send them texts?

No. It never calls or texts anyone first and makes no outbound calls, so it won't call leads back for the agent. It answers chats, browser calls and phone calls. The texts RealInfoBot sends are alerts to the agent and their team, and showing confirmations and reminders that buyers agreed to on a call. The agent can reply to a buyer's text by hand from the app.

Also asked as: outbound calls, call back leads, follow-up texts, text blasts, marketing texts, automatic follow-up, drip.

## What documents can the assistant learn from?

PDF, Word (DOCX), text and Markdown files, up to 25 MB each, such as the feature sheet, the listing description, or a condo or strata summary; text the agent types or pastes; and pages imported from a website address. A scanned PDF is read with text recognition, up to 25 pages. Each pack has room for its listings' documents: 20 MB on the 1-listing pack, 60 MB on the 3-listing pack and 120 MB on the 6-listing pack, counting only the text read from each file.

Also asked as: upload, file types, PDF, Word, photos, documents, storage, how much information, knowledge base size.

## How many listings and team members can I have?

Listings answering at once: 1 on the free plan and the 1-listing pack, 3 on the 3-listing pack and 6 on the 6-listing pack. A listing that sells can be paused and replaced by the next one.

Team members: plans don't charge per person. The owner invites people by email as admins or viewers, each with their own sign-in. An account can own up to 10 businesses, each with its own plan.

Also asked as: how many listings, number of users, seats, team size, multiple businesses, limits, extra users cost.

## Does RealInfoBot work when my phone is off?

Yes. The assistant runs in the cloud, not on the agent's phone, and answers calls to the RealInfoBot number and chats on the listing's page 24 hours a day. The agent's phone matters only for alerts and for calls put through to the agent's transfer number.

Also asked as: phone off, airplane mode, vacation, no signal, does it need my phone, always on, 24/7.

## When can I reach RealInfoBot's support team?

People answer at support@realinfobot.com, Monday to Friday, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. Mountain time, and aim to reply within 2 business days, often faster. The listing assistants answer buyers around the clock. Inside the app, a question mark on every screen opens its help page.

Also asked as: support hours, customer support, help, response time, contact support, email support.
