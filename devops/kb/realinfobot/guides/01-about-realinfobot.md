# About RealInfoBot

RealInfoBot gives a real estate listing its own AI assistant. Buyers scan the listing's QR code, open its web page or call its number, and the assistant answers their questions about the home, day or night, in their language. Every inquiry comes to the agent with a summary and the full transcript.

## What is RealInfoBot?

RealInfoBot is an AI assistant for real estate listings. You give it the facts about a home: the price, size, rooms, features, taxes, parking, schools and open house times, or your feature sheet and listing documents. It then answers buyers' questions from those facts by website chat, by voice in the buyer's web browser and on phone calls.

Each conversation that leaves a name or number comes to you in the RealInfoBot app, with a summary and the transcript, so you know who is interested and what they asked before you call back.

The idea in one line: one sign, one property, one voice. The sign on the lawn, the feature sheet and the listing's web page all lead to an assistant that knows that home.

## Who is RealInfoBot for?

RealInfoBot is for business use by real estate professionals: agents, teams and brokerages, and landlords and property owners who advertise a property for sale, for lease or for rent. You must be at least 18 to sign up, and the account is a business account, not a consumer one.

It suits solo agents who are at showings all day, teams that want one shared inbox for several listings, and anyone who wants buyers who drive by in the evening to get answers instead of a voicemail.

## Who makes RealInfoBot?

RealInfoBot is made by Real Info Box Inc., a Canadian company based in Calgary, Alberta. Real Info Box has served realtors since 2009. The app runs at https://ca.realinfobot.com and the website is https://realinfobot.com, with pages for Canada (https://realinfobot.com/ca) and the United States (https://realinfobot.com/usa).

## How do I contact RealInfoBot support?

Email support@realinfobot.com. A person reads every message. People answer Monday to Friday, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. Mountain time, while the listing assistants answer buyers around the clock. The Terms of Service say support aims to reply within 2 business days, and replies are often faster.

If you are already a customer, write from the email address on your RealInfoBot account so support can find it straight away. For a billing question, include your account email and your order number.

Write to the same address about pricing for a team or brokerage, questions before you buy, help with your account, privacy and personal information, a security issue, or press. The website's contact page is https://realinfobot.com/ca/contact.

## Can RealInfoBot set up my listing for me?

Yes. Setup takes about ten minutes if you do it yourself, and RealInfoBot can walk you through it or do it for you. Email support@realinfobot.com with the listing link and the best way to reach you, and the team will set up the listing's assistant, its QR code and its phone number with you, so you can try it before buyers do.

## What languages does RealInfoBot speak?

Buyers can ask in their own language. A phone call or browser call opens in the language you choose for your listing, and switches when a caller speaks another one, then keeps answering in it, with no menu to press. Website chats reply in the language the visitor writes in. Languages buyers use include English, French, Spanish, Chinese, Punjabi, Hindi, Korean and Ukrainian, among many others.

You write your listing facts once, in your own language. The assistant answers from them in the buyer's language and never adds anything you didn't give it.

The app itself, your dashboard, is available in English, French, Spanish and Serbian. The help center is in English.

## What does RealInfoBot not do?

- It never pretends to be a person, and it never calls or texts anyone first.
- It doesn't reply to text messages with AI. Texts to your listing's number wait in your Messages inbox, and you or your team reply by hand.
- It gives no opinions or advice: nothing on price negotiation, offers, the neighbourhood or the market, and no legal, financial, tax, mortgage or inspection advice. Those questions come to you.
- It isn't an emergency service.
- It doesn't print or ship anything. You download your QR code and signs and print them yourself.
- It doesn't act as a brokerage or a licensee for anyone.
