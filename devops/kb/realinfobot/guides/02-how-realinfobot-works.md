# How RealInfoBot works

How a buyer reaches a RealInfoBot listing assistant, what the assistant does, and how each inquiry reaches the agent.

## How does RealInfoBot work, step by step?

1. You add your listing in the RealInfoBot app at https://ca.realinfobot.com: type the facts, paste text from your feature sheet, or upload your listing documents.
2. Your listing gets a web page with a chat and a QR code that opens it. On a paid listing pack, your account also gets a phone number. You print the QR code, and the number if you have one, on your sign, a sign rider, a sticker or the feature sheet.
3. A buyer scans the code, opens the page or calls the number, and asks about the home. The assistant answers from your facts, in the buyer's language.
4. Each conversation lands in your app with a summary and the transcript, and the buyers who leave their details become contacts. You get alerts by email, by text or both, and you decide who to call back.

Setup takes about ten minutes. Buyers don't need an app: they point their phone camera at the code, or call the number.

## How can buyers reach my listing's assistant?

- **Scan the QR code.** It opens the listing's web page on the buyer's phone, with a chat. The buyer types a question, with no app to download. On a listing pack, they can also call the assistant from the browser and talk out loud.
- **Open the listing's page.** The same page works from a link you share anywhere: a social post, your email signature, a text to a buyer.
- **Call the phone number.** On a listing pack, the assistant answers calls to your RealInfoBot number, any hour. Your own phone number stays off the sign.
- **Chat on your own website.** Where the website widget is available to your account, you can add the chat to your own website with one line of code.

Texts to your RealInfoBot number are not answered by the AI. They wait in your Messages inbox, and you or your team reply by hand. See the RealInfoBot texts, alerts and notifications document.

## What happens during a call or chat?

The assistant tells callers it's an AI. Right after the greeting, every call says the caller is speaking with an AI assistant and the call is recorded and transcribed. Then it answers questions from the facts you gave it, in the caller's language.

When it doesn't know something, it says so and offers to take a message instead of guessing, and the question goes on your Questions list so you can answer it once. When someone wants a person, it takes their name and number and tells them you'll call back. On phone calls, it can also put the caller through to your own number if you set a transfer number. It can book showings or take showing requests if you turn booking on.

It never calls or texts anyone first, and it gives no opinions on price, offers, the neighbourhood or the market.

## What do I get after each conversation?

- **Calls** lists every call with its summary, transcript and recording.
- **Messages** is your inbox: texts to your number, website chats, and messages the assistant took on calls, such as a callback or a showing request.
- **Home** shows what needs a person under **Action required**, such as a callback the assistant took.
- **Contacts** builds itself from the people who call, text or chat and leave their details, with their calls, messages and transcripts.
- **Questions** lists what buyers asked that the assistant couldn't answer, with how many times each was asked.
- Alerts by email, by text or both tell you when a message or text comes in. Each person on your team chooses their own.

The contacts are yours. RealInfoBot doesn't sell them, share them or use them for anything else.

## Where does the assistant get its answers?

Only from what you give it: the knowledge you add for the listing (documents, text entries, answers you save on the Questions list and pages imported from a website), your AI settings and your services. It never answers from general knowledge or a guess. Rules you write tell it how to act, such as asking for a name and number before passing on a showing request.

A change to a text entry or a rule reaches the assistant within moments, for calls that start after you save it and for the next message in a chat. An uploaded document is used once its status on the Knowledge screen is **Ready**.

## Can my team and I reply to buyers ourselves?

Yes. You reply to texts by hand from your RealInfoBot number in Messages, and you can step into any open website chat with **Take over**, after which the assistant stops answering in that chat until someone selects **Resume AI**. Where live chat is turned on for the website widget, a visitor can also ask for a person, and your team has 3 minutes to join before the assistant takes the chat back and offers to take a message.

## Does RealInfoBot work at night and on weekends?

Yes. The listing assistant answers calls and chats 24 hours a day, every day, including when you've set opening hours: hours only decide when visitors can ask to chat with your team and when calls may be transferred under some transfer settings. Inquiries that come in overnight wait for you in the morning, with their summaries.

## Can RealInfoBot book showings?

Yes, when you set it up. On the **AI settings** screen, **Appointments** has three choices: **Books appointments**, where the assistant checks your open times and books the showing; **Takes requests**, where it notes the preferred time and the request reaches your team as a callback to confirm; and **Doesn't book**, where it takes a message instead. To book showings itself, the assistant needs at least one service that is turned on, such as a 30-minute showing, on the **Services** screen, and your opening hours on **AI settings**; until then it takes a message instead. Taking requests needs neither. On the **Integrations** screen you can connect Google Calendar, so the assistant doesn't book over your busy events and each booking is added to your calendar.

Also asked as: schedule a showing, book a viewing, appointment, open house booking, Google Calendar.
