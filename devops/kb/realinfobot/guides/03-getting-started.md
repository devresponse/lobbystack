# Getting started with RealInfoBot

How to sign up, what the setup steps ask, and a checklist for putting your first listing's sign up.

## How do I sign up for RealInfoBot?

Go to https://ca.realinfobot.com/en/signup, or select **Create one** on the log-in page.

1. Enter your email and a password that ticks every line of the checklist under it.
2. Complete the security check and select **Create account**.
3. Open the email from RealInfoBot and enter the 6-digit code. The code works for 10 minutes.
4. Select **Verify email**. The app signs you in and opens the first setup step.

Use an email address you can open right away and plan to keep: it's how you sign in and where your codes go. If a colleague invited you to their team, start from the link in their invitation email instead, so you join their business rather than creating a new one.

Signing up is free. You choose a plan during setup, and the free plan costs nothing.

Also asked as: create an account, register, open an account, join, start free, try it.

## What are the setup steps after I sign up?

Setup walks you through these steps, in order:

1. **Your business's name.** Use the name buyers know you by, such as your own name or your team's. It creates your business and makes you its owner.
2. **Your plan.** The free plan, or a listing pack. A pack opens a secure payment page, and setup goes on by itself once the payment is confirmed. See the RealInfoBot plans and pricing document.
3. **Your website.** Its address only, shown on your public page. This step doesn't read the site. Select **I don't have a website** to skip it.
4. **Training documents.** Upload your feature sheet or listing documents (PDF, Word, text or Markdown, up to 25 MB each), or paste the listing's facts as text.
5. **The greeting.** The first sentence the assistant says on every call.
6. **Your phone number.** Only on a plan that includes one, such as a listing pack: pick a local number. You can also choose **I'll add a number later**.
7. **How you heard about RealInfoBot.** One question, then you're in.

On the free plan, which has no phone number, setup skips the number step.

Setup takes about ten minutes.

Also asked as: how long does setup take, how long does it take to set up, onboarding steps, setup wizard.

## What should I have ready before I start?

- The listing's facts: price, size, bedrooms and bathrooms, features, taxes, parking, schools, open house times and anything buyers often ask.
- Your feature sheet or listing description as a PDF or Word file, if you have one.
- The phone number calls should be put through to when a buyer asks for you, if you want transfers.
- About ten minutes.

## How do I set up my first listing?

A quick-start checklist:

1. Sign up at https://ca.realinfobot.com/en/signup and create your business.
2. Add the listing: upload the feature sheet, or paste the facts. Add more on the **Knowledge** screen any time.
3. Check the facts buyers ask about: price, size, taxes, parking, schools and open house times.
4. Add two or three rules on the **Rules** screen, such as asking for a name and number before passing on a showing request.
5. Give your page its chat: create a widget on the **Website widget** screen, choose it under **Chat** on the **Public profile** screen, and select **Publish page** there, so the QR code opens a page buyers can chat on.
6. Print a sign or the code from the **QR code** screen, as a sticker, a sign rider or on the feature sheet.
7. Make a QR link for each place you put a code, so you can compare the yard sign with the feature sheet.
8. Choose your alerts on the **Notifications** screen: email, text or both. Invite your partner or assistant on the **Team** screen.
9. Test it yourself before the sign goes up: make a **Test Call** from the dashboard, scan the code and call the number.
10. Once a week, clear the **Questions** list, so each answer you add saves you a callback.

## How do I test my listing assistant before buyers use it?

Select **Test Call** at the top right of the dashboard to call the assistant from your browser and hear it answer. It isn't shown on a phone's small screen. When more than one AI agent is active, choose which one answers in the dropdown beside it.

Then scan your QR code with your phone to try the chat, and call your RealInfoBot number if you have one. A test call uses voice minutes like any call, and you can archive your test calls on their call pages to keep the Calls list tidy.

## What is the Getting started guide?

After setup, a **Getting started** card at the bottom of the sidebar shows your progress, such as 1 / 4 completed. It opens a guide of what's left so the assistant knows your whole listing: add more sources, read the rest of your website, hear your agent and connect a phone number. Each step ticks itself off when the app sees it done, and you can skip a step that doesn't apply. Once every step is done or skipped, the card leaves the sidebar.

## Can I set up several listings or businesses?

Yes. In RealInfoBot each listing is an AI agent in your business, and your plan decides how many can be active at once: one on the free plan and the 1-listing pack, 3 on the 3-listing pack and 6 on the 6-listing pack. Add them on the **AI agents** screen. See the RealInfoBot listings and AI agents document.

You can also own more than one business on one account, up to 10, for example a separate business for a team. **Create Business** in the business menu at the top of the sidebar starts the same setup for a new one. Each business has its own plan, knowledge, team and number.
