# RealInfoBot plans and pricing

RealInfoBot has a free plan and three listing packs you pay for once, with no subscription, plus a top-up of extra minutes. Prices are in Canadian dollars (CAD), and tax is added at checkout.

## Is RealInfoBot a subscription?

No. Each listing pack is paid once, lasts a set number of days from your payment, and then ends by itself. Nothing renews, there's no monthly bill, no overage bill and nothing to cancel. When the home sells, you simply stop: buy another pack when you take your next listing.

The free plan has no end date and costs nothing.

## What does the free plan include?

The free plan lets you set up and try RealInfoBot before you buy. Each month it includes:

- 30 voice minutes, for calls made from a web browser, such as your own test calls from the dashboard;
- 5 website chats;
- 10 alert text segments, the texts RealInfoBot sends for your business, such as alerts to you;
- 2 call transfer attempts;
- 1 listing (one AI agent);
- 1 MB of knowledge storage;
- no phone number, so buyers reach the assistant by chat, from your QR code and your listing's page. Calls from the page's chat need a phone number too, so they start with a pack.

The allowances start again on the first day of each month (UTC). When one runs out, that kind of use stops until the next month, or until you buy a pack or a top-up. The free plan's card lists community support, and you can still email support@realinfobot.com about your account. On the free plan, recordings, transcripts and messages are deleted after 30 days.

## What does the 1-listing pack include?

**Try it: 1 listing, 180 days**, CA$99 paid once. For 180 days from your payment, it includes:

- 1 listing (one AI agent);
- 250 voice minutes for phone calls and browser calls;
- 300 website chats;
- 300 alert text segments, the texts RealInfoBot sends you and your team;
- 120 call transfer attempts;
- 20 MB of knowledge storage;
- 1 phone number that takes calls and texts.

Its card reads: your sign answers every inquiry, day or night. It's active 180 days, then it expires.

## What do the 3-listing and 6-listing packs include?

**3 listings, 365 days**, CA$249 paid once, is everything in a single listing times three, for 365 days from your payment: up to 3 listings active at once, 750 voice minutes, 1,800 website chats, 1,800 alert text segments, 720 call transfer attempts, 60 MB of knowledge storage and 1 phone number.

**Pro: 6 listings, 365 days**, CA$449 paid once, is everything in a single listing times six, for 365 days: up to 6 listings active at once, 1,500 voice minutes, 3,600 website chats, 3,600 alert text segments, 1,440 call transfer attempts, 120 MB of knowledge storage and 1 phone number. It's made for agents who always have a sign up.

The minutes, chats, texts and transfers are shared by all the listings in the pack, not split per listing. The listings are how many AI agents can answer at the same time, not credits used up: when a home sells, archive or pause its AI agent and add the next listing in its place, for as long as the pack lasts.

## Does each listing get its own phone number?

No. Every pack includes one phone number for your account, which the free plan doesn't have. Each listing gets its own AI agent, its own web page and its own QR codes, so a buyer who scans a sign reaches the assistant for that home.

Calls to your one number are answered by the AI agent you choose for it on the **Phone Number** screen. With several listings, set it to your main AI agent, which can answer from every listing's facts, and callers say which home they're asking about. See the RealInfoBot calls and phone number document.

## Can I hold more than one pack?

Yes. You can buy another pack while one is still running, and their minutes, chats, texts and transfers add up. Each use draws on the pack that ends first. Each pack still ends on its own date, and what it holds then is lost, even while another pack runs, so there is no carry-over.

The listings, knowledge storage and phone number don't add up: you get the most that any one of your live packs gives. Two 1-listing packs still allow 1 listing at a time, so for more listings at once, choose the 3-listing or 6-listing pack.

A pack belongs to one business. If you own several businesses, each one needs its own.

## How do top-ups work?

A top-up adds extra allowance on top of your plan without changing it. RealInfoBot sells **100 extra minutes** for CA$39, paid once, which adds 100 voice minutes and lasts 180 days from your payment.

Top-ups are sold on the free plan and on listing packs. Owners and admins buy one under **Buy a top-up** on the **Plan** screen, or from the **Usage** screen. Calls use a top-up only once your plan's or packs' own minutes have run out, and the top-up that ends first is used first. A top-up never renews, isn't added automatically when minutes run low, and what it still holds when it ends is lost. It stays with your business if your plan or pack changes.

A top-up code from RealInfoBot works the same way, with no payment: add it under **Have a promo code?** on the **Plan** screen.

## Are prices in Canadian or US dollars? Is tax included?

The app shows and charges prices in Canadian dollars (CAD), for agents in Canada and the United States alike. Prices are shown before tax. Polar, RealInfoBot's merchant of record, adds the sales tax that applies, such as GST/HST or US sales tax, at checkout based on your location, and issues your receipt. Your bank may convert the amount if your card is in another currency.

## How are voice minutes, chats, texts and transfers counted?

- **Voice minutes** count the length of each call, on phone calls and on calls from a web browser, including your own test calls. Calls shorter than 10 seconds and calls ended as spam aren't counted. A call can last up to 30 minutes, with a warning a minute before the limit.
- **Website chats** count once per conversation, however many messages it has.
- **Alert text segments** count the texts RealInfoBot sends for your business: alerts to you and your team, the code that confirms a mobile number for alerts, and showing confirmations and reminders texted to buyers who agreed to them. A long text can take more than one segment.
- **Call transfer attempts** count each time the assistant tries to put a phone call through to your transfer number.

The **Usage** screen under **Settings** shows the voice minutes, transfer attempts, alert texts and knowledge storage used, and what's left.

## What happens when my minutes run out?

On a pack, a phone call gets a busy signal and a browser call shows that the limit is reached, until you buy another pack or a top-up. When transfers run out, the assistant takes a message instead of putting callers through; when alert texts run out, alerts aren't texted, though email alerts keep coming; when website chats run out, new chats stop.

Owners get an email when a fifth of a pack's balance is left and when none is left. On the free plan, the allowances come back on the first day of the next month.
