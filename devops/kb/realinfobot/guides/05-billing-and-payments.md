# RealInfoBot billing and payments

How to buy a listing pack or a top-up, who handles the payment, receipts, discount codes, what happens when a pack ends, and refunds.

## How do I buy a listing pack?

Owners and admins buy packs, on the setup's plan step or later from the app:

1. Open **Settings** in the sidebar and select the **Plan** tab, at https://ca.realinfobot.com/settings/plan.
2. Select **Upgrade plan** under **Your plan**. The **Upgrade plan** dialog shows the free plan and a card for each pack.
3. Select the pack's button, such as **Add it to your listing**, **Get 3 listings** or **Get 6 listings**.
4. Pay on the secure payment page that opens. Nothing is charged until you pay there.
5. When you come back, a message says the pack was added, and it shows under **Your plan**. If the payment is still processing, the pack shows once it arrives, usually within moments.

A business makes one purchase at a time: while one payment page is being prepared, another purchase for the same business is refused, so wait a minute and try again. A viewer on the team can see the plan but can't buy.

Also asked as: purchase, pay, checkout, upgrade, buy listings, add a listing pack, buy credits.

## Who processes RealInfoBot payments?

Polar (Polar Software, Inc.) is RealInfoBot's online reseller and merchant of record. Polar runs the secure payment page, charges your payment method, calculates and collects the sales tax that applies, such as GST/HST, VAT or US sales tax, and issues your receipt. RealInfoBot never receives your full card number.

For a billing question, contact RealInfoBot first at support@realinfobot.com. Polar can also be reached at support@polar.sh.

## Where do I enter a discount code for a pack?

Type it on Polar's payment page, in its discount code field, after you select the pack's button. The pack's card in the app keeps showing the full price, and the discount shows only on the payment page. The **Have a promo code?** link on the **Plan** screen doesn't take pack discount codes, so it calls them not valid.

Use **Have a promo code?** for a code that adds a top-up, such as free extra minutes from RealInfoBot: type it, select **Apply**, then **Add to my plan**. A top-up code can be added once to each business, and once by each person.

Also asked as: coupon, promo code, discount, voucher, offer code, code not valid.

## How do I get a receipt or an invoice?

Polar emails your receipt when you pay. On the **Plan** screen, owners and admins also see **Transaction history**: the business's 20 most recent orders and refunds, with the date, description, amount, status and an **Invoice** link where there is one.

If the payment page asks for a **Billing email**, the payment provider couldn't use your email for this business, often because it already handles another business's billing. Enter the email that should get this business's receipts and select **Continue**.

## When does my pack start and end?

A pack starts the moment your payment goes through and ends that many days later: 180 days for the 1-listing pack and 365 days for the 3-listing and 6-listing packs, counted as full 24-hour days. **Your plan** on the **Plan** screen shows the date it ends. With several packs, it shows **Prepaid Plan** and lists each one under **Your packs**, with its minutes left and its end date.

The owners get an email 30 days and 7 days before a pack ends, when a fifth of the balance is left, and when none is left.

## What happens when my last pack ends?

The business goes back to the free plan:

- Listings beyond the free plan's one are paused, the newest first. Nothing is deleted, so you choose which to resume when you buy again.
- Your phone number stops answering calls and texts at once, because the free plan has no number. The **Phone Number** screen gives the date it will be released, 30 days after the pack ended. Buying a pack before then makes the same number answer again; after that, the number is gone.
- The free plan's monthly allowances and its 1 MB of knowledge storage apply again. Your documents stay, but you can't add more while they're over the limit.
- Your listing page, QR codes and chat keep working within the free plan's allowances.

To keep a listing running, buy another pack before the end date. Packs never renew by themselves.

## Can I upgrade from the 1-listing pack to the 3-listing pack?

Buy the bigger pack whenever you need it, from **Upgrade plan** on the **Plan** screen. The packs you already hold keep running until their own end dates, and their minutes, chats, texts and transfers add to the new pack's, used from the pack that ends first. The number of listings becomes the new pack's, 3 or 6. There's no exchange or credit for the time left on a smaller pack.

## Can I get a refund?

RealInfoBot's Terms of Service give a full refund on a listing pack if you ask within 30 days of purchase, no questions asked, whatever was used. After 30 days no refund is given, including for unused minutes and for listings that sell, lease or are withdrawn early. If the service doesn't work as described because of a problem on RealInfoBot's side that can't be fixed within a reasonable time, RealInfoBot refunds the unused part.

To ask, email support@realinfobot.com with your account email and order number. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method through Polar. A refund of the whole payment ends the pack, and a partial refund takes its share of the pack's allowances off. The terms are at https://realinfobot.com/ca/legal/terms.

Also asked as: money back, refund policy, return, cancel my purchase, charge back.

## How do I cancel RealInfoBot?

Packs don't renew, so there's no subscription to cancel: a pack simply ends on its date. To stop using RealInfoBot completely, an owner can cancel the account on the **Team** screen with **Cancel account**, which closes the business without deleting anything, or delete a business there with **Delete business**, which erases it for good. See the RealInfoBot team, account and security document.

## A payment didn't go through, or the payment page expired. What can I do?

Select the pack's button again to open a new payment page, and pay there. If your card is declined, check its details or try another card on that page. If a message says the payment was refunded or charged back before it arrived, no pack was added: buy it again if you still want it, or contact support. If you paid but the screen still shows your old plan after a minute, reload the page and check **Transaction history**, then email support@realinfobot.com.

A pack's button can be greyed out with a note saying why. **Packs can't be bought here right now** shows when you aren't an owner or admin, or when packs can't be bought for the business at the moment. Too many tries in an hour also pause purchases: each business can start 10 purchases an hour, so wait and try again.
