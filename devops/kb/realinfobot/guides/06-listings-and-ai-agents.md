# RealInfoBot listings and AI agents

In RealInfoBot, each listing is an AI agent: an assistant with its own name, web page, QR codes and knowledge. The app's **AI agents** screen lists them, and the help pages also call an AI agent a receptionist.

## What is a listing in RealInfoBot?

A listing is one AI agent in your business that answers buyers about one property. Each one has:

- its own name, such as the street address, and a line saying what it helps with;
- its own web page, at your business page's address followed by its page address, such as `/42-aspen-crescent`;
- its own QR links and signs, made on the **QR code** screen, which open its page;
- its own greeting, language, transfer number and booking settings, on the **AI settings** screen;
- the knowledge you choose for it on the **Knowledge** screen.

Every business starts with one AI agent, its default one, named Front desk. On the free plan and the 1-listing pack, that one agent is your listing: give it your listing's facts and you're done.

Also asked as: AI agent, receptionist, assistant, bot, add a property, add a home, listing assistant.

## How many listings can I have at once?

Your plan decides how many AI agents can be active at the same time, the default one included: 1 on the free plan and on the 1-listing pack, 3 on the 3-listing pack and 6 on the 6-listing pack. Paused and archived agents don't count, so you can pause one to make room for another.

So on the 3-listing pack you have your default agent plus 2 more, or 3 listings if your default agent is one of them. The **AI agents** screen says how many are in use.

## How do I add another listing?

1. Open **AI agents** in the sidebar and select **Add receptionist**.
2. Enter a **Name**, such as the street address, and, if you like, **What it helps with**, such as "4-bedroom family home, open house Saturday 2 to 4".
3. Leave **Page address** empty to make it from the name, or type your own, such as `42-aspen-crescent`.
4. Choose its **Language** and **Knowledge**: **Articles for everyone, and the ones chosen for it** keeps other listings' facts out of its answers.
5. Select **Add receptionist**. It starts active.
6. Add the listing's facts on the **Knowledge** screen and make them **Available to** this agent only (see below), then make a QR link for it on the **QR code** screen.

Adding needs room on your plan: when every listing is in use, buy a bigger pack or pause one first.

## How do I keep each listing's facts separate?

Give each listing's documents and text entries to its own AI agent:

1. Set the listing's agent to answer from **Articles for everyone, and the ones chosen for it**, under **Knowledge** in **Edit details** on the **AI agents** screen. New agents start this way.
2. On the **Knowledge** screen, open each of the listing's articles, select **Available to** in its **More options** menu, choose **Chosen receptionists**, pick the listing's agent and select **Save changes**.

Articles left for **Everyone** are used by every listing, which suits facts about you, such as your brokerage or how to book a showing. Your default agent answers from every article unless you limit it, because it answers for anything that names no agent, such as your phone number. **Available to** decides what's relevant to each agent but doesn't keep anything private, so add only what buyers may hear.

## What happens to calls when I have several listings?

Your account has one phone number, and you choose the AI agent that answers it on the **Phone Number** screen. With several listings, let your default agent answer it: it knows every listing's facts, so callers just say which home they're calling about. With only one listing active, you can have that listing's agent answer the number.

QR codes and listing pages always reach the right listing, because each QR link names the agent that answers. Agents can't pass a call or a chat to each other: when a buyer asks about another of your listings, the agent names it and, in a chat, gives a link to its page.

Also asked as: two listings and one number, which house does a caller mean, one number for several homes.

## What do I do when a home sells?

Pause or archive its AI agent on the **AI agents** screen, in the agent's **More options** menu. Paused and archived agents stop counting toward your plan, so you can add your next listing in its place for as long as your pack lasts. Their calls, chats and contacts are kept.

A paused agent's QR links, phone number and widget answer as your default agent, and its own page sends visitors to your business page. You can also point an existing QR link at another agent on the **QR code** screen, so a sign rider you reuse opens the new listing. Take down signs you no longer use.

## Can I reuse a QR code or sign for my next listing?

Yes. QR links can't be deleted, but you can change which AI agent a link answers as, and rename it, on the **QR code** screen. A printed code keeps opening your page, now answered by the new listing's agent. This is handy for a sign rider or a permanent sign you move from home to home.

## What is the default AI agent?

The default agent answers your business page, your phone number, and any widget or QR link that names no agent. It stands in for an agent that is paused or archived, so a number or a printed code always reaches someone. It can't be paused or archived, always speaks the business's language and is always listed on your business page. To change which agent is the default, select **Make default** in another agent's **More options** menu.

## Can visitors choose between my listings?

Yes. On your business page, visitors choose who they're talking to under **You're talking to**, from the listings marked **Listed on the business page**. **Receptionists on your page** on the **Public profile** screen decides which ones show and in what order. Each listing also has its own page, which opens straight to its agent, and its page works even when it isn't listed. A listing's page opens for visitors once your business page is published.
