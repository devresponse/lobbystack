# RealInfoBot calls and phone number

How buyers call your listing's assistant, how to get and change your RealInfoBot phone number, transfers to your own phone, and what you see after each call.

## How do I get a phone number for my listing?

A phone number comes with every listing pack; the free plan has none. Pick it during setup, on the **Pick your AI's phone number** step, or later on the **Phone Number** tab in **Settings**, at https://ca.realinfobot.com/settings/phone-number.

The list shows local numbers in the country your business's time zone points to. You can search another country, and in the United States and Canada an area code, then select the number you want. Setting it up takes a moment. Every number on offer is a local number that takes both calls and texts, in the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom or Australia. A list of numbers is good for 5 minutes, and a number isn't held for you until you select it.

Also asked as: get a number, phone line, local number, area code, toll-free, choose my number.

## Does each listing get its own number?

No. Your account has one RealInfoBot number, included in your pack. Each listing has its own AI agent, page and QR codes, and you choose which agent answers the number on the **Phone Number** screen. With several listings, your default agent can answer it from every listing's facts, and callers say which home they mean.

## Can I change my phone number?

Yes, once. On the **Phone Number** tab, select **Request change** and pick a new number. The previous number keeps taking calls and texts for 30 days, so callers have time to learn the new one, and is then released. After one change, **Request change** is greyed out.

To prevent abuse, each person can try 3 numbers an hour, get 2 numbers in 24 hours and 5 in 30 days, across all their businesses.

## Can I use my own existing phone number?

The assistant answers calls on the RealInfoBot number you pick in the app; it doesn't take over your own mobile or office number. Your own number stays off the sign. You can have calls put through to your own phone when a buyer asks for you, by setting a transfer number.

## What does a caller hear?

The assistant answers with your greeting, the sentence you set on the **AI settings** screen, such as "Thanks for calling about 42 Aspen Crescent. How can I help?". Right after it, the call says the caller is speaking with an AI assistant and the call is recorded and transcribed, in the language the call starts in. Then the caller asks their questions out loud, and the assistant answers from your listing's facts, switching to the caller's language if they speak another one.

To change the greeting, edit **Greeting** on the **AI settings** screen and select **Save**. It reaches the assistant moments later, for calls that start after that. Hear it with **Test Call** at the top right of the dashboard.

Also asked as: welcome message, first thing the assistant says, change what callers hear when it picks up, intro, phone greeting.

## Can the assistant put calls through to me?

Yes, on phone calls. Enter your number, with + and the country code, in **Default transfer number** on the **AI settings** screen and select **Save**. When a caller asks for a person, the assistant puts the call through to that number. Leave it empty and select **Save** to stop transfers: the assistant then offers to take a message.

Each transfer counts toward the transfer attempts your plan includes: 2 a month on the free plan, 120 on the 1-listing pack, 720 on the 3-listing pack and 1,440 on the 6-listing pack. When they run out, the assistant takes a message instead. Calls made from a web browser, such as from your listing's page, can't be transferred: the assistant offers to take a message and gives your number to call.

## Can buyers call the assistant from my listing's page?

Yes, on a listing pack. The chat on your listing's page and on your website offers a call from the browser, where the buyer taps to talk to the assistant out loud with no phone line, when your business has a phone number and voice minutes left. Browser calls use voice minutes like phone calls, and can't be transferred to a person. On the free plan, which has no number, buyers chat, and your voice minutes are for your own test calls from the dashboard.

## How long can a call last?

Up to 30 minutes. The assistant warns the caller a minute before the limit and says goodbye politely. A call can also end sooner when your plan's remaining minutes run out.

## What do I see after a call?

The **Calls** screen lists every call, newest first, with who called, when, how long, which listing's agent answered and a summary. Select a call to open its page, with the transcript, the recording, the steps the call went through and any message or request the assistant took. A recording is usually ready a few minutes after the call ends.

Recordings, transcripts and summaries are kept for a limited time that depends on your plan: on the free plan, 30 days; on a listing pack, recordings and transcripts for 90 days and messages for 365 days. After that, the call stays in the list without them. Archiving a call hides it from the list without deleting anything.

## Can I block a nuisance caller?

Yes. Owners and admins can block a contact on their page on the **Contacts** screen. Calls from a blocked contact are hung up right away, and they get no texts from your business. Unblock them on the same page.

Also asked as: block a number, someone keeps calling, spam calls, robocalls, prank calls, unwanted callers.

## The assistant didn't answer my number. What's wrong?

Check, in order:

- **Minutes left.** A phone call gets a busy signal when your plan's minutes have run out. Check the **Usage** tab, and buy a top-up or another pack.
- **Your pack.** When your last pack ends, the number stops answering at once, and it's released 30 days later unless you buy another pack. The **Phone Number** tab says so with the release date.
- **Who answers.** On the **Phone Number** tab, check the agent in **Answered by**. A paused agent's number is answered by your default agent.
- **The number itself.** Call it from another phone. If the **Phone Number** tab shows no number, pick one.

If it still doesn't answer, email support@realinfobot.com with your number and the time of the call.
