# RealInfoBot texts, alerts and notifications

What happens when a buyer texts your RealInfoBot number, how you reply, and how you and your team get alerts by email and by text.

## Does RealInfoBot answer text messages?

No. RealInfoBot's AI never replies to texts. When a buyer texts your RealInfoBot number, the text lands in your **Messages** inbox, you get an alert if you've turned one on, and you or someone on your team replies by hand. Your reply goes out as a text from your RealInfoBot number, so your own number stays private.

The AI answers buyers by website chat, by browser call and on phone calls. RealInfoBot's SMS Terms say the same: it sends no marketing texts and doesn't reply to texts with AI.

Also asked as: SMS, text replies, texting, can buyers text the sign, auto reply to texts, answer texts automatically, text message bot.

## How do I reply to a buyer's text?

1. Open **Messages** in the sidebar. Each row is one conversation, the newest at the top.
2. Open the buyer's text thread.
3. Type your reply and send it. It goes out from your RealInfoBot number.

A reply can go out only when the person can get texts from you: they texted this conversation themselves or agreed to texts, they haven't texted STOP, they aren't blocked, and your business has a number that can send texts. When one of these is missing, the reply box is turned off and a notice says why.

The same inbox holds website chats and messages the assistant took on calls, such as a callback or a showing request, and you can reply to those callers by text too.

## What do STOP, START and HELP do?

A person who texts STOP gets no more texts from your business until they text START. A text that is only UNSUBSCRIBE, END, QUIT, CANCEL or STOPALL works like STOP, and one that is only UNSTOP or SUBSCRIBE works like START. So someone who texts just the word Cancel also stops getting texts. A person who texts HELP or START gets a standard automatic reply, which shows in the thread.

Also asked as: a buyer replied STOP, unsubscribe from texts, opt out of texts, opt back in.

## How do I get alerts about new inquiries?

Each person chooses their own alerts on the **Notifications** tab in **Settings**, at https://ca.realinfobot.com/settings/notifications, for each business they belong to.

- **Email** starts on for everyone who joins the team. Alerts go to the address you sign in with.
- **SMS** sends alerts by text to your mobile. The first time you turn it on, you confirm your mobile number with a 6-digit code, then tick the alerts you want in the **SMS** column, which starts with nothing ticked.

The alerts you can choose include **Voice message captured**, when a caller leaves a message during a call, and **New text message**, when someone texts your number. Alerts about website chats, **New website chat message** and **Visitor asking for a person**, are set on the **Website widget** screen. There are also alerts for system issues, such as **Live call transfer failed** and **Calendar sync issue**.

There is no save button: each change saves as soon as you make it.

Also asked as: notifications, notify me, lead alerts, inquiry alerts, text me when someone calls, email me.

## Can I get an email after every call?

Yes. Under **Communication** on the **Notifications** tab, turn on **Call summary** or **Call transcript** for yourself. Owners and admins choose, under **Emails after each call**, who can get it (owners and admins, everyone on the team, or nobody) and what it holds: **Summary and a link**, the default, which keeps the conversation behind sign-in, or **Summary and the whole conversation**. The email goes to everyone who turned it on and is written in each reader's dashboard language. Replying to it writes to an owner of the business, not to the caller.

## Do alert texts cost anything?

Alert texts count toward the alert text segments your plan includes: 10 a month on the free plan, 300 on the 1-listing pack, 1,800 on the 3-listing pack and 3,600 on the 6-listing pack. A long text can take more than one segment. The code that confirms your mobile number counts too. When they run out, alerts aren't texted until you get more, but email alerts keep coming. Your carrier's message and data rates may apply, and you can reply STOP to an alert text to stop them.

## Why is the SMS switch greyed out?

It says "Unavailable. This workspace has no alert SMS sender." when the business has no number that alert texts can be sent from, for example on the free plan, which has no phone number. Use email alerts for now. Text alerts become available once the business has its own phone number, which comes with a listing pack. Alert texts come from your RealInfoBot number.

## I don't get alert emails or texts. What can I check?

- Check that **Email** or **SMS** is on under **Channels** on the **Notifications** tab, and that the alert is ticked in that column.
- Alert emails go to the address you sign in with, shown on the **Account** screen. Look in that inbox and its spam folder.
- Your choices apply only to the business you have open, so check them in each business.
- For texts, check that you confirmed your mobile number and haven't replied STOP to an alert.
- Check the **Usage** tab: alert texts stop when their allowance runs out.
