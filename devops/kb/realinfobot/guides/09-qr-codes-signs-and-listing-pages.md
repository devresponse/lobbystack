# RealInfoBot QR codes, signs and listing pages

How your listing's web page works, how to make QR codes and printable signs, where to put them, and how scans are counted.

## What is my listing's page?

Your business has a public web page, and each listing's AI agent has its own page at that address followed by the listing's page address. The page shows your name, what you do, your phone number, your address with a map, your hours and services if you choose, and a chat with the listing's assistant, where buyers type a question or call the assistant from their browser.

Set it up on the **Public profile** tab in **Settings**, at https://ca.realinfobot.com/settings/profile: add a category, a public email, a description and an address, and choose what the page shows. The page stays private until you select **Publish page**: until then only people signed in to your team see it, as a preview. A published page can be found by search engines, and visitors can switch it between English, French, Spanish and Serbian.

Share the link anywhere buyers look: your social profiles, your email signature, a text to a buyer, or a QR code.

Also asked as: public profile, landing page, listing website, web page, profile page, share link, publish.

## How do I put the chat on my listing's page?

The page's chat runs on a website widget. On the **Public profile** screen, choose the widget under **Chat** and select **Save**. If you have none yet, create one on the **Website widget** screen first. The chat shows once the page is published, even if the page's address isn't in the widget's allowed websites. Without a chat, a page that shows a phone number offers a panel to call your assistant instead.

If the **Website widget** screen says new widgets aren't available yet, email support@realinfobot.com.

## How do I get a QR code for my listing?

Open the **QR code** tab in **Settings**, at https://ca.realinfobot.com/settings/qrcode. Your business page has a code of its own, but its scans aren't counted. For each sign you put up, make a link instead:

1. Select **New link**.
2. Enter a **Label**, such as where the sign will go: `Yard sign` or `Feature sheet`.
3. With several listings, choose the listing's agent in **Answered by**.
4. Select **Create link**. The link's sign shows below the table.
5. Print the sign, or download the code.

Each link has its own short address and code, opens the page of the agent that answers it, and counts its scans. Publish your page on the **Public profile** screen before you put signs up: codes open your public page, though you can print them before that.

## How do I print a sign?

In the sign card on the **QR code** tab, choose:

- **Template**: **Signal**, a yellow sign; **Front desk**, white, which uses less toner; or **Night**, black, for dim places.
- **Message**: the words printed on the sign, up to 80 characters.
- **Size**: **Full page · A4** for 1 sign, **Half page · A5** for 2, **Quarter page · A6** for 4 or **Cards · A7** for 8 on a sheet. Each says from about how far its code scans.
- **Inside the code**: the app's name, its logo or nothing.
- **Language**: English, French, Spanish or Serbian.

Select **Print**, and print at actual size (100%), not Fit to page, on A4 paper. Matte paper scans best, and **Save as PDF** in the print window makes a PDF to send to a print shop. To use the code in your own design, such as a sign rider, a sticker or the feature sheet, select **Code as PNG**, or **Code as SVG** for a file that stays sharp at any size. Scan the printed sign with two phones before you put it up.

RealInfoBot doesn't print or ship anything: you print the code yourself or with your usual sign company.

## Where should I put my listing's QR code?

Six places that catch buyers when they're curious:

1. **The yard sign.** A sticker or sign rider turns every drive-by into a possible conversation, day or night. If you do only one thing, do this.
2. **The open house door or sign-in table.** Visitors too shy to ask in front of others can scan on the way back to the car.
3. **The feature sheet.** Sheets go home with people, so kitchen-table questions still get answered that evening.
4. **A social post.** Add a picture of the code, or your number on a pack.
5. **Your email signature.** "Questions about this home? Scan or call" with the page link or number.
6. **The front window.** On a busy street, a window card reaches people walking past.

Make a separate QR link for each placement, named after where it goes, so you can see which one buyers use. On the sign, say "Scan to chat" so buyers know they can ask any time.

## How are scans counted?

Each QR link counts its scans for each day, kept for 13 months. On the **QR code** tab, **Scans, 30 days** shows each link's last 30 days, and selecting its row shows **Scans per day**. Days follow UTC time. Scans count only while your page is published, and checking a link yourself with **Open page** isn't counted. Your business page's own code isn't counted, so use links for your signs.

A business can keep up to 1,000 links. Links can't be deleted, so a printed code keeps working. To reuse one, open its **More options** menu, select **Edit** and change its **Label** or the agent in **Answered by**: the code stays the same. **Pause** makes a link open your business page instead, until you resume it.

## My QR code doesn't work. What can I check?

- **The page isn't published.** A link's code says the QR code doesn't open a page, and the business page's code says the page doesn't exist, until you select **Publish page** on the **Public profile** screen.
- **The link is paused.** A paused link opens your business page. Resume it in the link's **More options** menu.
- **The print is too small or blurry.** Print at actual size, on matte paper, and check the scanning distance given for the size you chose.
- **There's no chat on the page.** Choose a website widget on the **Public profile** screen.
