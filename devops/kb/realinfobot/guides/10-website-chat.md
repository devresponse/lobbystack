# RealInfoBot website chat

How the chat with your listing's assistant works on your listing page and on your own website, how your team joins a chat, and how chats are counted.

## Where can buyers chat with my listing's assistant?

On your listing's public page, which your QR codes open, and on your own website if you add the chat there. Both run on a website widget, made on the **Website widget** screen, at https://ca.realinfobot.com/settings/widget. The chat on your public page uses the widget you choose on the **Public profile** screen.

The assistant answers chats from your listing's facts, in the language the visitor writes in, in short replies. It can ask for the visitor's contact details, book showings the way it does on calls, and, on a listing pack, offer a call from the browser.

## How do I add the chat to my own website?

1. Open **Website widget** in the sidebar.
2. Under **Create a widget key**, enter a **Label**, such as your website's name.
3. In **Allowed origins**, enter your website's address, such as `https://example.com`. Add every address the site uses, separated by commas: an address with www and one without it are different, and so are http and https.
4. Choose the color, position, words and options you want.
5. Select **Create widget key**, then **Copy embed snippet** and **Done**.
6. Paste the snippet, one short line of code, into every page that should show the chat, or into the place your website builder takes custom code for all pages, and publish your site. You can also send the snippet to whoever manages your website.
7. Open your website, select the chat button and send a test message. The chat shows on the **Messages** screen.

Only the websites in **Allowed origins** can run your chat. Changes to a widget reach your website without a new snippet. With several listings, each widget names the AI agent that answers it.

If the **Website widget** screen says new widgets aren't available yet, email support@realinfobot.com.

## Can my team join a website chat?

Yes. Every website chat shows on the **Messages** screen, where your team can read it and reply. Writing in a chat takes it over, and the assistant stops answering there until someone selects **Resume AI**, or until the visitor writes after your team has been quiet for 10 minutes.

With **Let visitors chat with your team** turned on for the widget, a visitor can also ask for a person. The assistant stops answering, your team gets an alert, and has 3 minutes to reply or select **Join chat**; after that, the assistant takes the chat back and offers to take a message. Visitors can ask for a person only during the opening hours set on **AI settings**, or at any time when no hours are set. They see the first name of the person who joins.

## Can the chat collect buyers' contact details?

Yes. On the widget's card, under **Lead form**, tick **Enabled**. You can make email or phone required, and ask before the chat starts. A visitor who leaves an email address or phone number is added to your **Contacts**, or matched to the contact that already has it. A name alone doesn't make a contact.

## How are website chats counted?

A website chat counts once per conversation, however many messages it has: 5 a month on the free plan, 300 on the 1-listing pack, 1,800 on the 3-listing pack and 3,600 on the 6-listing pack. When they're used up, chats the assistant already answers keep getting replies, but it doesn't answer new ones: those visitors are told your team will reply, and their messages still reach **Messages** and still send alerts.

To protect your allowance from automated messages, the assistant starts at most a quarter of your chats in one day, never fewer than 10, and starts again at midnight UTC. Before it answers, the chat quietly checks that the visitor is a person; now and then it asks them to tick a box.

## Which chat alerts can I get?

At the bottom of the **Website widget** screen, each person chooses their own alerts for website chats: **New website chat message**, which comes at most once per chat each day, **Visitor asking for a person**, and **Website chat reached its daily limit**. The **Email** and **SMS** switches are the same as on the **Notifications** screen.

## When does a website chat close?

A chat closes when the visitor writes again after a day without messages, and their next message starts a new chat. It also closes when the visitor starts chatting with a different listing's agent, unless your team has the chat or the visitor is waiting for your team.
