# Teaching your RealInfoBot assistant: knowledge, rules and questions

How to give your listing's assistant its facts, how to tell it how to act, how to answer the questions it missed, and what it will and won't say.

## How do I add my listing's facts?

On the **Knowledge** screen, at https://ca.realinfobot.com/agent/knowledge, in three ways:

- **Upload a document** you already have, such as your feature sheet, the listing description, a disclosure summary or a strata or condo summary. The upload takes PDF, Word (DOCX), text and Markdown files, up to 25 MB each, one at a time.
- **Import your website's pages**, such as the listing's page on your own site, starting from the address you enter. An import reads up to 50 pages, and each page becomes its own entry.
- **Write a text entry**, such as "Open house: Saturday 2 to 4 pm" or "Property taxes: $4,850 for 2026".

A text entry reaches the assistant within moments. A document or website page is used as soon as its status is **Ready**. Facts you added during setup are here too, including pasted text as an entry called **Onboarding notes**.

Also asked as: knowledge base, upload feature sheet, add listing details, training documents, teach the bot, add information, import website.

## What should I tell the assistant about a listing?

Write the facts as completely as you can, in your own language. Buyers most often ask about:

- the price, and whether it's still available;
- size, bedrooms, bathrooms, the basement, the garage and the yard;
- property taxes, condo or strata fees, and what they include;
- parking, schools, transit and what's nearby;
- the year built, updates, heating and cooling;
- open house times and how to book a showing;
- possession date and what's included in the sale.

Keep each text entry to one topic. Put long material in a document. When a detail changes, such as a new open house time or a price change, update the entry: the assistant uses it from the next call or chat.

## Why didn't the assistant know something I uploaded?

- **It's still being read.** Check the document's status on the **Knowledge** screen: it's used once it shows **Ready**.
- **It couldn't be read.** A problem shows under the document. A scanned PDF, whose pages are only pictures, is read with text recognition up to 25 pages, works best on English and French text, and a longer scan can't be read.
- **It isn't available to that listing.** With several listings, check **Available to** in the article's **More options** menu, and the agent's **Knowledge** choice on the **AI agents** screen.
- **It's turned off.** Turned-off entries and documents aren't used.
- **The storage is full.** On the **Usage** tab, a full **Knowledge base** bar means new documents and pages can't be added.

Documents can't be edited in the app: upload the new version and delete the old one.

## How much knowledge can I add?

Document and website text is limited by your plan: 1 MB on the free plan, 20 MB on the 1-listing pack, 60 MB on the 3-listing pack and 120 MB on the 6-listing pack. Only the text read from each file counts, not the file's size, so a 10 MB PDF of photos with a page of text takes very little. Documents that are turned off count too. Text entries don't count, but each listing uses up to 50 text entries that are turned on. **Knowledge base** on the **Usage** tab shows how much is used.

## What are rules?

Rules tell the assistant how to act; knowledge tells it what to say. Write them on the **Rules** screen in plain words, the way you'd brief a new assistant, one situation per rule. Rules agents find useful:

- "Before passing on a showing request, ask for the buyer's name and best number."
- "If someone asks about offers or price negotiation, say the agent will call them back."
- "If someone asks about the open house, mention that it's this Saturday from 2 to 4."
- "If a caller says they're a real estate agent, ask for their brokerage."

The assistant reads rules from the top, so put the most important first. It follows the first 50 rules that are turned on and reads up to 4,000 characters of each; calls read the rules that fit, from the top. A new or changed rule reaches the assistant moments after you save it. Rules apply to every listing, and don't apply to texts, which your team answers.

## What is the Questions list?

When a buyer asks something the assistant can't find in your facts, it says it doesn't have that detail, offers to take a message, and adds the question to the **Questions** screen, with how many times it was asked. Answer it there once, and your answer becomes a text entry on the **Knowledge** screen, so the next buyer gets it within moments. Dismiss a question that doesn't need an answer.

After a call, up to five unanswered questions from it are added; website chats add theirs as the chat goes on. Clear the list once a week: after a week or two, most common questions are covered.

## What will and won't the assistant say?

The assistant follows four promises in every conversation and every language:

1. **It says it's an AI.** Callers hear that they're speaking with an AI assistant and that the call is recorded and transcribed. It never pretends to be a person, and it never calls or texts anyone first.
2. **It only knows what you told it.** It answers from your knowledge and settings, never from general knowledge or a guess. If it doesn't know, it says so and offers to take a message.
3. **It gives no opinions.** No comments on price, offers, the neighbourhood or the market, and no legal, financial, tax, mortgage or inspection advice. Those questions come to you.
4. **It passes people who want a person to you.** It takes their name and number and tells them you'll call, or puts a phone call through to your transfer number if you set one.

Your rules work inside these promises. AI can still make mistakes, so read your conversations, and fix a fact in a minute when an answer was off.

## How do I fix a wrong answer?

Open the conversation on the **Calls** or **Messages** screen to see what was said. Then correct or add the fact on the **Knowledge** screen, or write a rule on the **Rules** screen if it's about how the assistant acted. The change reaches the assistant moments later. If the answer came from an old document, upload the new version and delete the old one.

## What is the business summary?

The business summary is what the assistant tells callers about you in a few sentences. The AI writes it from the articles available to every listing and rewrites it as they change, until you write your own under **Business summary** on the **AI settings** screen; after that it stays as you wrote it.
