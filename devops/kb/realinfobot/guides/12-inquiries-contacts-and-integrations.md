# RealInfoBot inquiries, contacts, analytics and integrations

Where buyer inquiries land in the RealInfoBot app, how contacts build themselves, what the analytics show, and how to connect other tools.

## Where do I see new inquiries?

- **Home** gathers what needs a person under **Action required**: a callback or showing request the assistant took on a call, a cancellation request, a website chat waiting for your team, and the count of unanswered questions. The numbers at the top compare calls and bookings in the last 30 days with the 30 days before.
- **Calls** lists every call with its summary, transcript and recording.
- **Messages** is the team's inbox: text threads with your number, website chats, and messages the assistant took on calls.
- **Questions** lists what buyers asked that the assistant couldn't answer.

Each inquiry shows the buyer, a summary such as "asked about the price and a Saturday showing", the full transcript and the listing it was about. You decide who to call back first. The app updates by itself as calls and messages come in.

Also asked as: leads, inquiries, inbox, where are my messages, who called, follow up, callbacks.

## How do contacts work?

**Contacts** lists the people your assistant talked to. A contact is added by itself when someone calls or texts your number for the first time, calls from a browser, books a showing, or leaves an email or phone number in a website chat. Calls and texts from the same number are grouped on one contact. Open a contact to see their whole history: their calls, messages, transcripts and appointments.

You don't type contacts in: details come from the conversations, such as the caller's name after a call. Owners and admins can block a contact, which stops texts to them and hangs up their calls, and delete a contact that has no history. With several listings, the list shows everyone together.

The contacts are yours. RealInfoBot doesn't sell them, share them or use them for anything else. A buyer who wasn't right for one home might be right for your next listing.

## Can I export my contacts or calls?

The app has no export button on the Contacts or Calls screens. You can read calls, contacts and messages from your own tools with an API key, a webhook, or a connected AI assistant, or email support@realinfobot.com for help.

Also asked as: export leads, download contacts, CSV, spreadsheet, Excel, back up my data.

## What does the Analytics screen show?

**Analytics** turns calls, messages and bookings into charts: how busy the listing is, how calls end and how buyers reach you, across phone calls, browser calls, texts and website chats. Choose the dates and how to group them at the top. The cards show the totals, charts over time and a comparison with the same number of days just before. For scans of each sign, see the **QR code** screen.

## Can I connect RealInfoBot to Google Calendar?

Yes. On the **Integrations** screen, select **Connect** on the **Google Calendar** card, sign in to Google and choose a calendar. The assistant then doesn't book showings over the busy events on that calendar, and every showing booked, moved or cancelled is added to, changed on or removed from it. All your listings use the same calendar. RealInfoBot uses your calendar only to find free times and to manage the showings the assistant books, and event titles and descriptions are never sent to AI providers.

## Can I connect RealInfoBot to my CRM or other tools?

Yes, with the tools on the **Integrations** and **Settings** screens:

- **Webhooks** send events, such as a finished call or a new message, to your own systems as they happen. Manage them from the **Webhooks** card on **Integrations**.
- **API keys**, on the **API keys** tab in **Settings**, let another tool or a script work with your business without signing in as a person, with only the access you choose. The whole key is shown once, so store it somewhere safe.
- **Connected apps** lists the AI assistants an owner or admin connected to the business, such as one that reads calls or adds knowledge for you. Disconnect any you no longer use.

The **Zapier** and **Make** cards show only where RealInfoBot offers its own Zapier integration or Make app; where they don't show, use webhooks and API keys.

Also asked as: CRM, Follow Up Boss, kvCORE, Zapier, Make, API, webhook, integrate, sync leads.

## What is the Preferences tab?

**Preferences** in **Settings** holds your own display choices: the **Dashboard language** (English, French, Spanish or Serbian), the theme and the time format. They change only what you see, not what the assistant says to buyers. The app writes its emails to you in your dashboard language.
