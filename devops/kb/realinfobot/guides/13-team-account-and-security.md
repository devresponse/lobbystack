# RealInfoBot team, account and security

How to sign up and sign in to the RealInfoBot app, reset a password, invite your team, own several businesses, and cancel or delete a business.

## How do I create a RealInfoBot account?

Open https://ca.realinfobot.com/en/signup, which the **Add it to your listing** buttons on https://realinfobot.com/ca open, or select **Create one** on the app's log-in page.

1. Enter your email address and a password: 8 to 128 characters, with at least one number and one special character, such as `!`, `#` or `?`.
2. Complete the security check below the form and select **Create account**.
3. Enter the 6-digit code the app emails you, and select **Verify email**. The app signs you in and starts the setup.

A code works for 10 minutes and allows three wrong tries, and only the newest code works. The app sends at most one code a minute and three an hour to the same address. Your account takes the language the sign-up page is shown in.

If someone invited you to their team, start from the link in the invitation email instead, so you join their business rather than creating a new one.

Also asked as: sign up, register, create account, verification code, new account, get started.

## How do I sign in, and how long do I stay signed in?

Open https://ca.realinfobot.com, enter your email and password and select **Sign in**. If your address isn't confirmed yet, the app emails you a 6-digit code to enter. You stay signed in on the same browser until you sign out, or until 30 days pass without using the app. Sign out from the menu with your email address at the bottom of the sidebar.

After several tries in a minute, the app answers **Incorrect email or password** even when both are right: wait a minute and try again. If you keep coming back to the log-in page, allow cookies for the app's address.

Also asked as: log in, login, can't sign in, locked out, wrong password, sign out, log out.

## I forgot my password. How do I reset it?

1. On the log-in page, select **Forgot password?**.
2. Enter your email and select **Send reset code**.
3. Enter the 6-digit code from the email and a new password, then select **Reset password**.

The app signs you in with the new password and signs you out on every other device. RealInfoBot resets passwords with a code, not a link, so a page opened from a reset link can't change your password. A code works for 10 minutes and allows three wrong tries. No email arrives when no account uses the address, so check the address and your spam folder.

Also asked as: password reset, forgotten password, reset code, recover account, first password.

## How do I change my email address or password?

Open the menu with your email address at the bottom of the sidebar and select **Account**, at https://ca.realinfobot.com/settings/account. Your sign-in details are yours, not a business's, so a change applies in every business you belong to.

- **Email**: select **Change**, enter the new address and select **Send confirmation link**. Approve the change from the email sent to your current address, then confirm it from the email sent to the new one. Each link works for 1 hour. Until then you keep signing in with the old address.
- **Password**: select **Change**, enter your current password and the new one twice, and select **Save**. This signs you out on every other device and browser, which is also the way to lock out anyone who knows your old password.

If you don't remember your current password, sign out and use **Forgot password?**.

## How do I add my team, or an assistant or partner, to RealInfoBot?

Owners and admins invite people on the **Team** tab in **Settings**, at https://ca.realinfobot.com/settings/team: select **Invite**, enter one email address, choose a role and select **Send invitation**. Each person signs in with their own account, so nobody shares a login. Plans don't charge per team member.

- **Admin** can invite and remove people and change the business's settings and billing.
- **Viewer** can see calls, contacts and activity without changing anything.

The person must sign in, or create an account, with the address the invitation went to. An invitation link works for 7 days; one that ran out still shows as **Pending**, so cancel it and send a new one. Each person can send 20 invitations an hour.

Also asked as: invite, add user, team members, staff access, give my assistant access to my account, partner, co-listing agent, roles, permissions, admin, viewer.

## How do I change someone's role or remove them?

The **Team** tab has no role switch. To change a role, open **More options** on the person's row, select **Remove**, and invite the same address again with the new role. To remove someone, select **Remove**: there's no confirmation step, and they lose access at once. Removing a member also cancels their pending invitations, revokes the API keys they created for this business and stops the apps they connected.

Owners and admins both show as **Admin** in the list, and only owners can delete the business or cancel the account. Owners can't be removed from this screen, and a business always keeps at least one owner. To make someone else the owner, the business is handed over through the app's API with a key that has `team:manage` access. Email support@realinfobot.com for help with it.

Also asked as: remove user, change role, make owner, transfer ownership, hand over the business.

## Can I have more than one business on one account?

Yes, up to 10 businesses on one account, for example one for yourself and one for a team. **Create Business** in the business menu at the top of the sidebar starts the setup for a new one, and the same menu switches between them. Each business has its own plan or packs, phone number, listings, knowledge, contacts and team. For several homes, you usually don't need several businesses: one business holds several listings with a 3-listing or 6-listing pack.

## What's the difference between canceling the account and deleting a business?

Both are for owners, on the **Team** tab.

- **Delete business** erases a business and everything in it for good: calls and recordings, chats, contacts, appointments, knowledge, settings, billing history and the team's access. Its phone number is released, and its listing page and chat stop working. Nothing can be restored, by you or by support. You type the business's name and your password to confirm. The first business on your account can't be deleted this way.
- **Cancel account** closes the first business on your account, once it's the only business you own, without deleting anything. Its phone number stops answering, and is released after 30 days; its listing page, chat, API keys and connected apps stop working; and nobody on the team can open it. You confirm from a link emailed to you, which works for 24 hours, by entering your password.

Canceling or deleting doesn't pay back a pack by itself; refunds follow the refund policy in the RealInfoBot billing and payments document. People keep their own sign-ins either way, and you can start again later with a new business.

Also asked as: close account, delete account, cancel service, stop RealInfoBot, remove my data, deactivate.

## How is my account kept secure?

- Each person signs in with their own email and password, and confirms their address with an emailed code.
- Sign-in, reset and invitation codes and links expire quickly, and repeated tries are slowed down.
- Changing or resetting your password signs you out everywhere else.
- Changing your sign-in email needs approval from both your old and new inboxes.
- Deleting a business needs your password, and canceling needs a confirmation from your email.
- API keys show their full value only once, and can be revoked on the **API keys** tab in **Settings**.

The app has no separate two-factor sign-in with an authenticator app or a text code. If you get an email approving a change you didn't ask for, ignore it, then change your password on the **Account** screen. For anything that looks wrong with your account, email support@realinfobot.com.
