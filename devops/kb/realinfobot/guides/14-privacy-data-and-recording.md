# RealInfoBot privacy, data and call recording

Who owns the conversations and contacts, call recording and disclosure, how long records are kept, which AI and other providers process data, security, and privacy requests. The full Privacy Policy is at https://realinfobot.com/ca/legal/privacy.

## Who owns my contacts and conversations?

You do. The calls, chats, messages and contacts your listing's assistant collects belong to your business. RealInfoBot uses them only to provide the service to you, and for security, billing and the law. It doesn't sell them, share them for advertising or use them for its own marketing. It doesn't share phone numbers, text opt-in data or consent records with anyone for their marketing.

For the buyers who scan your sign or call your number, you, the agent or owner of the listing, decide how their information is used, and RealInfoBot processes it on your behalf as your service provider.

Also asked as: data ownership, my leads, do you sell data, who sees my contacts.

## Are calls recorded, and do callers know?

Yes. Every call is recorded and transcribed, and the assistant says so: right after your greeting, the caller hears that they're speaking with an AI assistant and that the call is recorded and transcribed, in the language the call starts in. Many places require telling callers that a call is recorded. Owners and admins can turn the sentence off with **Tell callers about AI and recording** on the **AI settings** screen, but only do that if you tell callers another way.

Your team can play and download recordings from the **Calls** screen. A recording is usually ready a few minutes after the call ends.

Also asked as: call recording, consent, recording disclosure, recorded line, is this call recorded, turn off the recording message.

## How long does RealInfoBot keep recordings, transcripts and messages?

- **Free plan:** recordings, transcripts and messages are deleted after 30 days.
- **Listing packs:** recordings and transcripts are deleted after 90 days, and buyer inquiries and messages after 365 days.
- **Account information** is kept while the account is open, then deleted or de-identified. Billing and tax records are kept as long as the law requires, and backups expire on their normal cycle.

After its recording and transcript are gone, a call stays in the **Calls** list without them. To keep a recording longer, download it from the call's **Recording** tab.

Also asked as: data retention, how long are calls kept, recording deleted, transcript missing.

## Can I delete a call, a message or a contact?

- **Calls and messages:** archiving hides them from your lists without deleting anything. They are deleted by themselves at the end of the retention period above.
- **Contacts:** owners and admins can delete a contact that has no history, and block one who shouldn't reach you.
- **Knowledge:** documents and entries can be deleted on the **Knowledge** screen at any time.
- **Everything:** an owner can delete a whole business on the **Team** tab, which erases its calls, recordings, chats, contacts, knowledge and settings for good.

To have particular records deleted sooner, email support@realinfobot.com.

Also asked as: delete recording, delete transcript, erase a buyer's data, remove a contact, right to be forgotten.

## Which AI does RealInfoBot use, and is my data used to train AI?

Conversations are handled by OpenAI models. Voice audio goes to OpenAI in real time so the assistant can listen and reply, and OpenAI keeps a copy of voice recordings for 30 days under its own policies. RealInfoBot sends AI providers only what the task needs, such as the conversation and the listing's facts.

RealInfoBot doesn't use personal information to train AI models, and its providers' business terms don't allow them to train on it. If you connect Google Calendar, event titles and descriptions are never sent to AI providers.

Also asked as: OpenAI, ChatGPT, which AI model, AI training, is my data used for training, LLM.

## Which companies process data for RealInfoBot, and where?

RealInfoBot is a Canadian company. It and its providers process information in Canada, the United States and other countries where the providers operate. The providers, called subprocessors:

- **OpenAI:** AI conversations, chat replies, summaries and call recordings for 30 days.
- **Twilio:** phone numbers, calls, transfers and text messages.
- **Railway:** hosting, databases and file storage, including recordings.
- **Cloudflare:** website hosting, security and bot protection.
- **PostHog:** website and product analytics, with consent.
- **Polar:** checkout, billing and tax, as merchant of record.
- **Google:** calendar access, only when you connect Google Calendar.
- **Firecrawl:** reading your public website, only when you import it.
- **Resend:** account and alert emails.
- **PayPal:** affiliate payouts.
- Monitoring and logging providers.

The list is kept at https://realinfobot.com/ca/legal/subprocessors.

Also asked as: subprocessors, data location, where is my data stored, data residency, Canada, third parties.

## How does RealInfoBot protect my data?

RealInfoBot's safeguards include encryption in transit, encryption of sensitive credentials, separation of each customer's data, restricted role-based staff access, and logging. Google Calendar tokens are encrypted and deleted when you disconnect. Each person on your team has their own sign-in, and you choose who is an admin or a viewer.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach creates a real risk of significant harm, RealInfoBot notifies you and the regulators as the law requires.

## Which privacy laws does RealInfoBot follow, and how do I make a request?

RealInfoBot follows PIPEDA, Alberta's PIPA and other applicable Canadian laws, including Quebec's Law 25. For the EEA and UK it relies on contract, legitimate interests, consent and legal obligation, and uses standard contractual clauses for transfers. Where US state privacy laws apply, you can ask to know, access, correct or delete your information.

Depending on where you live, you can access or get a copy of your information, correct it or ask for it to be deleted, object to or restrict some processing, withdraw consent, and complain to a privacy regulator. Email support@realinfobot.com, attention of the Privacy Officer. RealInfoBot verifies your identity first and replies within the time the law requires, usually 30 days.

Also asked as: PIPEDA, GDPR, CCPA, Law 25, privacy request, access request, privacy officer.

## A buyer wants to know how their information is used. What do I tell them?

A buyer's questions, contact details and conversation records go to you, the agent or owner of the listing, who controls them, so privacy requests come to you first. If a buyer writes to RealInfoBot instead, RealInfoBot passes the request to you and helps you answer it. To stop texts from your RealInfoBot number, the buyer replies STOP.

Buyers who chat on your listing page or website keep a random visitor identifier in their browser, so a conversation continues if they reload the page. When you add the chat to your own website, your website is responsible for any cookie notice it needs.

## What is the Share telemetry data switch?

On the **Preferences** tab in **Settings**, **Share telemetry data** lets the app collect anonymized usage and error data from your business, such as which screens are opened, to help improve the app. It starts on, it applies to the whole business, and only owners and admins can turn it off or back on. On the website, analytics cookies run only after a visitor accepts them, and **Cookie preferences** in the footer changes that choice.

Also asked as: telemetry, analytics, tracking, cookies, opt out of analytics.
