# RealInfoBot quick answers

Short answers to common questions about RealInfoBot that the other documents don't cover on their own.

## Is there a free trial? Do I need a credit card to start?

The free plan is the way to try RealInfoBot, with no end date. Choose it on the setup's plan step and no payment is needed: you set up one listing, build its page and QR code, and test the assistant with your own calls from the dashboard. Payment details are asked only when you buy a listing pack, on Polar's secure payment page. The free plan has no phone number, so buyers reach its assistant by chat.

Also asked as: trial, free trial, credit card required, try before I buy, demo account.

## Can I hear the assistant before I sign up?

Yes. The RealInfoBot website, https://realinfobot.com/ca, has a sample listing's sign to scan or call, so you can talk to a listing assistant on your phone. After you sign up, **Test Call** at the top right of the dashboard calls your own assistant from the browser.

## Is there a contract or a commitment?

No. There's no contract, no subscription and nothing that renews. A listing pack is paid once and ends on its date, and the free plan costs nothing. You can stop using RealInfoBot whenever you like.

Also asked as: contract, commitment, lock-in, cancel anytime, monthly fee.

## Do I need technical skills to set it up?

No. Setup takes about ten minutes in the browser: name your business, add your listing's facts by typing, pasting or uploading a document, choose a greeting and, with a pack, pick a phone number. Adding the chat to your own website takes one line of code, which you or whoever manages your site pastes in. RealInfoBot support can also help: email support@realinfobot.com.

Also asked as: do I need to be technical to use this, is it easy to use, I'm not good with computers, do I need a web developer.

## Is there a RealInfoBot mobile app?

There's no app to install. The dashboard at https://ca.realinfobot.com works in your phone's browser, with the sidebar behind the menu button at the top. Alerts reach you by email or text wherever you are. Buyers don't need an app either: they scan the code with their phone camera or call the number.

Also asked as: iPhone app, Android app, app store, use on my phone, mobile.

## Can I choose the assistant's voice or name?

You choose what the assistant says first, its greeting, on the **AI settings** screen, and the language calls start in. The app doesn't offer a choice of voices. Each listing's AI agent has a name you set on the **AI agents** screen, such as the home's address, which visitors see when they choose a listing on your business page.

Also asked as: change voice, male or female voice, accent, assistant name, rename the bot.

## What is a receptionist in the RealInfoBot app?

Some screens and messages in the app call your listing's AI agent a receptionist, as in **Add receptionist** or **All receptionists**. It's the same thing: each listing has its own AI agent, which answers calls and chats about that home. The **AI agents** screen lists them.

Also asked as: receptionist, AI agent, assistant, bot, listing assistant, front desk.

## Does RealInfoBot connect to MLS, REALTOR.ca or Zillow?

There's no direct connection to MLS boards or listing portals. You give the assistant its facts by typing them, pasting text, uploading your feature sheet or other documents, or importing pages from a website address on the **Knowledge** screen, such as the listing's page on your own site. To send inquiries to a CRM or other tool, use webhooks, API keys or a connected AI assistant, from the **Integrations** and **Settings** screens.

Also asked as: MLS, REALTOR.ca, Zillow, listing feed, IDX, import listing automatically.

## What kinds of properties can I use it for?

RealInfoBot is built for real estate agents' listings. The assistant answers only from the facts you give it, so it works for the home or property you describe on its listing, along with its open house times and how to book a showing. Each listing has its own AI agent, page and QR codes.

Landlords and property owners use it for a property for rent or for lease, too.

Also asked as: rentals, rental property, leasing, condos, townhouses, apartments, open houses.

## Does the assistant work when my phone is off?

Yes. The assistant runs in the cloud, not on your phone, and answers calls to your RealInfoBot number and chats on your listing page 24 hours a day. Your phone only matters for the alerts you choose and for calls put through to your transfer number.

## Can several callers reach the assistant at the same time?

Each call and chat is its own conversation, so a buyer doesn't wait for another buyer to finish. Every call uses your plan's voice minutes, and every new website chat counts toward your chats.

Also asked as: can two buyers call at the same time, several calls at once, simultaneous calls.

## What is the free RealInfoBot guide?

A free 16-page guide for real estate agents: why people stop at your sign, setting up a listing in about ten minutes, how inquiries reach you, what the assistant will and won't say, and places to put your listing's code besides the sign. Leave your name and email at https://realinfobot.com/ca/freereport to get it by email.

Also asked as: free report, guide, PDF, ebook, how-to guide.

## I run a team or brokerage. Can RealInfoBot work for us?

Yes. Invite your team on the **Team** tab, each person with their own sign-in, as an admin or a viewer, and use the 3-listing or 6-listing pack for several listings at once. For a whole team or brokerage, email support@realinfobot.com to talk about what you need.

Also asked as: brokerage, team plan, many agents, enterprise, office.

## Can I resell RealInfoBot or use it for my clients?

Not without a written agreement with Real Info Box. The Terms of Service and Acceptable Use Policy don't allow reselling, renting or offering the service to others without one. To talk about it, or about the affiliate program, which pays you for referrals, email support@realinfobot.com.
