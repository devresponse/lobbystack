## Who can use RealInfoBot?

RealInfoBot is for business use only: real estate professionals, teams, brokerages, landlords and property owners who advertise a property for sale, lease or rent. You must be at least 18 and the age of majority where you live. Accepting the terms for an organization binds that organization. RealInfoBot isn't directed to anyone under 16.

## What am I responsible for as the listing's agent?

The Terms of Service treat the assistant like a flyer or feature sheet that buyers can ask questions: you decide what goes in it, and you are responsible for what it says.

- Enter only information you're allowed to share, including any consent the seller or landlord must give, and keep it accurate and current.
- Deactivate listings that are sold, leased, withdrawn or expired.
- Never enter lockbox or access codes, alarm or security details, or schedules of when occupants are away.
- Follow the rules where you work: licensing, brokerage supervision, advertising, disclosure and AI disclosure rules, and fair housing, human rights and anti-discrimination laws. Don't set up the assistant to refuse, steer or treat visitors differently on any protected ground.
- Test each assistant before you put the QR code out.

RealInfoBot doesn't review, verify or approve your listing content.

## What do the terms say about the AI's limits?

The assistant always identifies itself as an AI and never claims to be a person. It's designed to answer only from your listing content, and refers what it can't answer to you. AI can still be wrong or incomplete, and RealInfoBot doesn't guarantee every answer. RealInfoBot is not a brokerage or licensee and doesn't act for any buyer, seller, landlord or tenant. The assistant is set up not to give pricing opinions, valuations, offer recommendations, negotiation advice, comments on other offers or seller motivation, opinions on neighbourhoods or the market, or legal, financial, tax, mortgage or inspection advice, and you must not set it up to.

The assistant tells visitors the conversation is handled by AI and recorded. You are responsible for any further notice or consent the law requires where you or your visitors are, including call recording, all-party consent and AI disclosure laws. Visitor contact details are given to you so you can answer that visitor's inquiry: any further message you send is yours and must follow anti-spam and privacy laws such as CASL, PIPEDA, the TCPA, CAN-SPAM and the GDPR. RealInfoBot doesn't send marketing messages for you. Don't use the service to collect payment card numbers, government ID numbers or health information.

Also asked as: consent, recording laws, CASL, TCPA, follow-up emails, marketing to buyers.

## What are the rules for phone numbers and texts?

Phone numbers and texts come through carriers such as Twilio. You don't own a number RealInfoBot provides, and it can be released when your purchase ends. Texts sent by the service are transactional only: alerts to you and your team, showing reminders buyers agreed to, and verification codes. RealInfoBot doesn't send marketing texts and doesn't reply to texts with AI. Recipients can reply STOP to opt out and HELP for help. Add only alert recipients who are your staff or contractors and who agreed to get alert texts.

No. RealInfoBot can't contact emergency services or handle urgent situations, so don't let anyone rely on it for emergencies.

You own your listing content and the visitor information collected for your listings.
