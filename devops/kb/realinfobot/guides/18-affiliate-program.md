# RealInfoBot affiliate program

How to earn a commission by referring other agents to RealInfoBot: your referral link, how referrals are credited, and when and how commissions are paid.

## What is the RealInfoBot affiliate program?

Everyone who signs in to RealInfoBot has their own affiliate program. Share your personal referral link with other agents, and when a business you referred pays for RealInfoBot, you earn 20% of each payment it makes in its first 12 months. The program belongs to you as a person, not to a business: your link, earnings and payout email are the same whichever business you have open.

Open it with the gift icon labelled **Affiliate Program** at the top right of the dashboard, at https://ca.realinfobot.com/affiliate. On a phone, where the icon doesn't show, open that address in the browser.

Also asked as: affiliate, referral program, refer a friend, partner program, earn money, commission, referral link.

## How is a referral credited to me?

1. Someone follows your referral link to the sign-up page. The visit counts as a click.
2. They sign up and create their business in the same browser. That business is credited to you.
3. For 12 months from then, every payment that business makes earns you a 20% commission.

Each business can be credited to one affiliate only, and businesses you create yourself don't count, even through your own link. Tracking doesn't work in a browser that blocks sites from saving data, as some private windows do.

## When and how are commissions paid?

Each commission waits through a 30-day holding period, in case the payment is refunded, and then becomes eligible. Eligible commissions are paid monthly through PayPal, once they reach the minimum payout of $100, to the PayPal email you save on the **Settings** tab of the affiliate screen. Save that email before your first commission becomes eligible, because payouts leave out anyone without one. It can be different from the address you sign in with.

If a payment is refunded or disputed before its commission is paid, the commission is reduced, or shows **Voided** when nothing is left of the payment.

Also asked as: payout, PayPal, minimum payout, holding period, when do I get paid.

## What does the affiliate screen show?

- **Referral link**, with **Copy link**.
- **Rewards**: the rate, the commission period, the minimum payout and the holding period, with a link to the terms.
- **Clicks**, **Referrals** and **Conversions**: visits through your link, businesses credited to you, and payments that earned a commission.
- **Pending**, **Eligible** and **Paid**: commission still in its holding period, commission ready for the next payout, and everything paid so far.
- **Earnings** and **Payouts**: your latest commissions and payouts, with dates and status.

## How should I share my referral link?

Put it where agents look, such as your newsletter, a post in an agent group or a message to a colleague who asked, and show the sign working by letting them scan your sign or call your listing's number. Describe RealInfoBot honestly and say that you may earn a commission: the affiliate screen asks the same of everyone.
