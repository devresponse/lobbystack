# RealInfoBot Plan and Usage screens

What the **Plan** and **Usage** tabs in **Settings** show in RealInfoBot, where you buy listing packs and top-ups and follow what's left. RealInfoBot sells the free plan, listing packs paid once and top-ups, with no subscriptions, so these screens never show monthly billing, a spending cap or a subscription to manage.

## What does the Plan screen show?

Open **Settings** in the sidebar and select the **Plan** tab, at https://ca.realinfobot.com/settings/plan. Everyone on the team can see it, and owners and admins can buy.

- **Your plan**: Free, or your pack's name, price and the date it ends. With several packs it says **Prepaid Plan** and the date the last one ends.
- **What's included**: what your plan or packs include: voice minutes, how many listings can be active at once, transfer attempts, alert texts and knowledge storage.
- **Your packs**: with several packs, each one with its minutes left out of its total and its end date.
- **Top-ups**: extra minutes or other allowances on top of your plan, with what's left and when each ends.
- **Buy a top-up**: the **100 extra minutes** card with its **Buy** button, for owners and admins.
- **Have a promo code?**: where a top-up code from RealInfoBot is added.
- **Upgrade plan**: the button that opens the free plan and the listing packs.
- **Transaction history**: the business's 20 most recent orders and refunds, with **Invoice** links, for owners and admins.

There's no **Manage subscription** button, because nothing renews: Polar emails your receipt when you pay.

Also asked as: plan page, billing page, my plan, current plan, subscription page, plan tab.

## How do I buy a pack from the Plan screen?

Select **Upgrade plan** under **Your plan**. The dialog shows the free plan and a card for each pack, each with its price, how long it lasts, what it includes and a button: **Add it to your listing** for the 1-listing pack, **Get 3 listings** and **Get 6 listings** for the others. Select one and pay on Polar's secure payment page, where a discount code can be entered. When you come back, a message says the pack was added, and it shows under **Your plan**. While you hold packs, the free plan's card reads **When your packs end**, because the business returns to it then.

A greyed out button has a note under it saying why it can't be bought now, such as when you aren't an owner or admin.

## How do I buy extra minutes?

Owners and admins select **Buy** on the **100 extra minutes** card under **Buy a top-up** on the **Plan** tab, or **Buy a top-up** on the **Usage** tab, and pay on the secure payment page. When you come back, **Your top-up was added.** shows and the top-up appears under **Top-ups**. It costs CA$39, adds 100 voice minutes, and lasts 180 days from your payment. A top-up is used only once your plan's or packs' own minutes have run out.

Also asked as: buy minutes, more minutes, top up, extra minutes.

## How do I use a promo code?

For a top-up code from RealInfoBot, such as free extra minutes: select **Have a promo code?** under **Your plan**, type the code, select **Apply**, check what it adds and how long it lasts, then select **Add to my plan**. No payment is needed, and **Top-up added.** confirms it. A code can be added once to each business and once by each person.

For money off a pack, use the discount code field on Polar's payment page after you select the pack's button. **Have a promo code?** doesn't take pack discount codes and calls them not valid.

## What does the Usage screen show?

The **Usage** tab is the first tab of **Settings**, at https://ca.realinfobot.com/settings/usage. It shows figures only, for the whole business, shared by all its listings:

- **Voice minutes**: minutes spent on phone calls and browser calls, including your test calls, out of what's included.
- **Transfer attempts**: calls the assistant tried to put through to your transfer number.
- **SMS alerts**: alert texts, codes that confirm a phone for alerts, and showing confirmations and reminders texted to buyers, counted in segments.
- **Knowledge base**: the space the text of your documents and website pages takes, in MB, out of your plan's storage.
- **Top-ups**: a line under a bar, such as **Top-ups: 45 of 100 min left**, while a top-up of that kind runs.

Website chats aren't on this screen. On the free plan, **Resets on** gives the date the monthly allowances start again, the first day of each month in UTC. On packs, the screen says your allowances come from your packs and last until each one ends.

Also asked as: usage page, minutes used, minutes left, how much is left, quota, balance, remaining minutes.

## What does No minutes left or Limit reached mean on the Usage screen?

The allowance is used up. On packs, **No minutes left** for voice minutes, or **None left** for transfers and texts, stays until you buy another pack or a top-up: phone callers hear a busy signal, the assistant takes a message instead of transferring, and alert texts aren't sent, though email alerts keep coming. On the free plan, **Limit reached** lasts until the date after **Resets on**. While a top-up still holds some of that kind, the line carries on from the top-up instead.

A full **Knowledge base** bar means new documents and website pages can't be added. Delete documents you no longer need, or buy a pack with more storage. Turning a document off doesn't free space, and text entries don't count.

## Why don't the voice minutes match my calls?

Calls shorter than 10 seconds and calls ended as spam don't count, while test calls from the dashboard and browser calls from your listing page do. A call in progress holds some minutes while it runs, and the count is corrected to its real length when it ends. Minutes are rounded to a tenth of a minute.
