# Know the decisions

The maintainer's decisions about the fork, newest first, with where each is carried out. A decision stands until the maintainer changes it; to change one, say so in the pull request that changes it and update this page. Architecture decisions inherited from the original project are its [ADRs](#read-the-architecture-decisions), and billing's design choices are in [billing design](../billing/design-history.md#know-the-decisions).

| 2026-11-09 | A business can buy prepaid minute packs, as an additional option that changes nothing for a business that never buys one: no plan, allowance, price, checkout or bill moves. The provider only takes a one-time payment, and LobbyStack keeps the minutes, their end date and what each call drew. The maintainer's eight answers: 1. Each pack has a length in days, and 90, 180 and 365 are on sale. 2. No carry-over: each pack ends on its own date, and calls draw from the pack that ends first. 3. A pack gives Starter's limits (one number, one receptionist) with 20 MB of knowledge, and Starter's monthly allowances of alert texts, transfers and website chats, counted per UTC month. 4. At zero minutes a phone call gets a busy signal, as on Free, and a website call shows the limit. 5. No refund by default: refunds are made in the provider's dashboard, and LobbyStack shrinks or freezes the pack from the provider's event. 6. A business can switch both ways: a subscription that starts ends its packs, and a pack bought by a subscriber ends its subscription; minutes and allowances left at the switch are gone, and the new side starts fresh allowances. 6a: no pack for a subscriber on a yearly plan until it ends. 6b: no pack while a payment is past due. 6c: fresh allowances only for a business switching to or from a pack. 7. Sizes of 250, 500 and 1,000 minutes, each size and length its own entry in `billing.json` with its own Stripe price or Polar product. 8. Built on `feature/receptionists` only, as migration `0148`.
