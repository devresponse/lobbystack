Open **Agent** > **AI settings** to edit the basic behavior used by the receptionist.

## Greeting

The greeting is the opening line your receptionist uses when answering a call or starting a conversation. Keep it short and specific.

Good greetings usually include the business name and a clear offer to help. You don't need to say that the caller is speaking with an AI: the call says so right after the greeting ([Tell callers about AI and recording](#tell-callers-about-ai-and-recording)).

| Business type | Example greeting |
| --- | --- |
| Dental clinic | Thanks for calling Bright Smile Dental. How can I help? |
| Salon | Thanks for calling North Loop Salon. I can help with services, availability, and messages for the team. |
| Home services | Thanks for calling Peak HVAC. Are you calling about service, scheduling, or an urgent issue? |
| Restaurant | Thanks for calling Maple Table. I can help with hours, reservations, and messages for the staff. |

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open AI settings">
    In the dashboard sidebar, go to **Agent** > **AI settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the greeting">
    Update the **Greeting** field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save** beside the greeting field.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tell callers about AI and recording

Right after the greeting, every call says: "You're speaking with an AI assistant, and this call is recorded and transcribed." It's said in your **Default customer language**, on phone calls and website calls alike, and the call button on your website shows a line that says the same.

It's on for every business. Many places require telling callers that a call is recorded, so turn **Tell callers about AI and recording** off only if you tell callers another way, such as in your greeting. The change applies from the next call.

## Business summary

The business summary is what your receptionist tells callers when they ask what the business does or offers. It reads the summary before every call.

You don't have to write it. When you add your website or documents in **Knowledge**, AI writes a summary of two to four sentences from them, in your default customer language. It rewrites the summary a few minutes after your knowledge changes, and only when the content changed.

To write your own, edit the **Business summary** field and click **Save**. AI never changes a summary you wrote. To go back to the AI summary, click **Rewrite with AI**.

## Opening hours

Your receptionist books appointments only inside your opening hours. With instant booking on and no hours set, it can't offer a time. It takes the caller's request as a message for your team instead, and the booking settings show a warning until you add hours.

You don't have to type them in. When your website or documents state your hours, AI fills in **Opening hours** a few minutes after your knowledge changes. It reads hours in any language and saves a lunch break as two windows on the same day. It only uses hours your sources state, and a note above the hours tells you to check them.

To set the hours yourself, turn each day on or off and enter its times. Click **Add hours** for a second window around a break, then click **Save hours**. AI never changes hours you saved here or through the API.

## Default customer language

Calls start in the **Default customer language**, and call summaries are written in it. Choose one of 13 languages, each shown in its own name:

| Option | Value |
| --- | --- |
| English | `en` |
| Español | `es` |
| Français | `fr` |
| Deutsch | `de` |
| हिन्दी | `hi` |
| 日本語 | `ja` |
| 한국어 | `ko` |
| Português | `pt` |
| 中文（普通话） | `zh` |
| Italiano | `it` |
| Українська | `uk` |
| Русский | `ru` |
| العربية | `ar` |

Write your greeting in the same language, since it's the first thing callers hear. The notice after it, about AI and recording, is always in this language.

Appointment texts follow the contact's language, or else this one. They're written in English, French, Spanish, and Serbian, and go out in English for any other language.

<Note>
  This setting is the language calls start in, not a limit on conversation language. When a caller asks for another language or clearly speaks one, the receptionist switches and keeps answering in it for the rest of the call. Add knowledge, service, and rule content in the languages your callers are most likely to use.
</Note>

## Default transfer number

The **Default transfer number** is the phone number used when the receptionist performs a live handoff to a human.

Use a number that is answered during business hours. If different teams handle different requests, keep the default number as the safest general handoff and add rules for when a caller should be transferred.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter a number">
    Add a complete phone number in the transfer number field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save** beside the transfer number field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add rules if needed">
    Use **Agent** > **Rules** to describe when the receptionist should transfer, take a message, or ask for callback details.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Add a transfer number when you want live human handoff. Without one, the receptionist takes callback or follow-up details so your team can respond from the dashboard.
</Note>

## Appointment changes

Use **Appointment changes** to choose whether the AI receptionist can cancel or reschedule appointments automatically.

You can also require a texted one-time code or a keypad PIN before any appointment is changed.
