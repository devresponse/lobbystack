LobbyStack supports six plan states in the app:

- **Free** for hosted workspaces with capped included usage.
- **Starter** for hosted workspaces with a monthly or annual subscription, included usage, and billable overages.
- **Pro** for hosted workspaces with higher included usage, monthly or annual billing, and billable overages.
- **Prepaid** for hosted workspaces that pay once for a pack of voice minutes instead of a subscription.
- **Enterprise** for custom hosted arrangements.
- **Self-host** for deployments that use your own providers and infrastructure.

## Hosted plans

| Plan | Monthly charge | Annual charge | Voice minutes | Alert SMS segments | Transfer attempts | Knowledge base | Overages |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | - | 30 browser min | - | - | 1 MB | No |
| Starter | $30/month | $288/year ($24/month effective) | 150 min | 50 | 20 | 5 MB | Yes |
| Pro | $100/month | $960/year ($80/month effective) | 500 min | 200 | 100 | 20 MB | Yes |
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom | Custom | Custom | Custom | Custom | Custom |

Free has no phone number. You test the receptionist with browser calls, so Free can't send texts or transfer calls. Starter and Pro include one business number.

A transfer attempt is the second phone leg LobbyStack places when it transfers a live caller to a person. LobbyStack doesn't make any other outgoing calls.

Paid plan overage rates:

| Usage type | Starter | Pro |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Voice | $0.20 per extra minute | $0.18 per extra minute |
| Alert SMS | $0.02 per extra segment | $0.02 per extra segment |
| Transfer attempts | $0.02 per extra attempt | $0.02 per extra attempt |

Included usage resets monthly on monthly and annual subscriptions. Unused minutes do not roll over.

## Prepaid minute packs

A prepaid pack is one payment for voice minutes that last a set number of days from the day you pay, instead of a monthly subscription. A pack doesn't renew and never bills overages. Calls stop at zero minutes, as on Free. Phone calls then get a busy signal until you buy another pack.

Each pack ends on its own date, and any minutes left on it then are lost, even if you hold another pack. Calls use the pack that ends first. While a pack lasts, **Settings** > **Plan** shows your plan as **Prepaid**, with the minutes left and the date your last pack ends. Alert texts and transfer attempts count against the monthly allowances listed there and pause when they run out.

The workspace owners get an email when about a fifth of the minutes are left, when none are left, and 30 and 7 days before a pack ends. Once your last pack ends, your business number stops answering calls and is released 30 days later. Buying another pack or a plan before then keeps the number, and it answers again.

### Buy a pack

Owners and admins can buy packs, except on Enterprise:

1. In the dashboard, open **Settings** > **Plan**.
2. Under **Prepaid minutes**, choose the **Minutes** and how long the pack **Lasts**.
3. Click **Buy pack**, or **Buy more** if you already have a pack, then pay on the payment page. The pack shows in **Settings** > **Plan** once your payment arrives.

During onboarding, you can also buy a pack under **Prefer prepaid minutes?** in the plan step.

### Switch between a pack and a plan

You can switch either way, but the minutes and allowances left at the switch are lost. The new plan's or pack's allowances start right away.

- Buying a pack on a monthly Starter or Pro plan ends the plan as soon as the pack is paid. You confirm with **End plan and buy** first. The rest of the plan's month isn't refunded, and its usage so far is still billed.
- Subscribing to Starter or Pro ends your packs.
- A yearly plan can't switch to a pack. Set it to end at its renewal, then buy a pack once it ends. You also can't buy a pack while a plan payment is past due. Settle it under **Manage subscription** first.

## Manage billing

In the dashboard, open **Settings** > **Plan** to:

- See the current plan.
- Upgrade to Starter or Pro.
- Buy a [prepaid minute pack](#prepaid-minute-packs).
- Enter a promo code under **Have a promo code?** to see the plan it unlocks, what it includes each month and its discount, then subscribe to it. A promo plan can include more minutes, texts or storage than the plan it is based on, and **Settings** > **Plan** and **Settings** > **Usage** then show its own allowances. A promo link fills the code in for you. Each workspace can use a code once, so after a promo plan ends, its code doesn't sell it again. A promo plan that lasts a set number of months shows when it ends and what comes next, either its base plan at the standard price or the end of the subscription, and the owners get an email a week before. It ends at the first renewal after its months run out, and changing your plan before then ends it and its discount.
- Manage your subscription.
- Review recent billing transactions.
- Set a monthly overage spending cap.
