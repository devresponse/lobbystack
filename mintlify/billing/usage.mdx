The **Settings** > **Usage** page shows hosted usage for the current month. Usage resets on the first of each month (UTC), whatever day your subscription renews.

## Usage metrics

| Metric | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Voice minutes | Time spent on live voice calls. Calls under 10 seconds and calls the receptionist ends as spam are excluded. |
| Alert SMS segments | SMS notification segments sent by the system. |
| Transfer attempts | Outgoing call legs to your team when the receptionist transfers a live caller. LobbyStack makes no other outgoing calls. |
| Knowledge base | Text the receptionist searches, extracted from your documents and website imports. File sizes don't count. |

## Included usage

| Metric | Free | Starter | Pro |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Voice minutes | 30 browser min | 150 min | 500 min |
| Alert SMS segments | - | 50 | 200 |
| Transfer attempts | - | 20 attempts | 100 attempts |
| Knowledge base | 1 MB | 5 MB | 20 MB |

## How limits work

Free has no phone number, so it can't send texts or transfer calls. Free hosted workspaces include a set usage pool for each billing period. When an included pool is used up, that usage type pauses until the next period or until the workspace upgrades.

Starter and Pro support billable overages for voice, alert SMS, and transfer attempts. Knowledge base has a fixed plan allowance; remove old content or upgrade before adding more after the allowance is full.

On Starter and Pro, you can set a **Monthly spending cap** under **Settings** > **Plan**. The cap pauses only usage that would take this month's overage spend past it. Minutes, texts and transfer attempts your plan still includes keep working. Paused usage resumes next period, or as soon as you raise the cap.

## Overage rates

| Usage type | Starter | Pro |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Voice | $0.20 per extra minute | $0.18 per extra minute |
| Alert SMS | $0.02 per extra segment | $0.02 per extra segment |
| Transfer attempts | $0.02 per extra attempt | $0.02 per extra attempt |

Monthly and annual subscriptions both reset included usage on the first of each month (UTC), and only usage past it is billed. Unused included minutes do not roll over.

## View usage

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    In the dashboard, open **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Usage">
    Select **Usage** from the settings sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review included usage">
    Check current usage, remaining quota, usage status, and reset timing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review pay-as-you-go usage">
    On Starter or Pro, open **Plan** to see this month's estimated overage spend under **Monthly spending cap**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Displayed overage amounts are estimates for the current period until the final invoice is created.
</Note>
