The **Settings** > **Notifications** page controls alerts for the active business. You can enable email, SMS, or both for events that need staff attention.

## Choose notification channels

Enable the channels your team monitors:

- **Email**: Sends alerts to the account email address
- **SMS**: Sends urgent alerts to the verified operator phone number

SMS alerts require explicit consent. Review [SMS alerts and consent](/integrations/sms) before enabling them.

## Choose alert events

LobbyStack can notify your team about:

- A voice message that requires a callback
- A new text message sent to your business number
- A new website chat that needs attention
- A website visitor asking for a person, when [live chat with your team](/settings/widget#live-chat-with-your-team) is on
- A failed customer SMS notification
- A calendar synchronization issue
- A failed live-call transfer
- A call that ended, with its summary or the whole conversation ([Get an email after each call](#get-an-email-after-each-call))

Enable alerts that require a person to act. Keep routine delivery events disabled if your team does not need them.

## Get an email after each call

Each person can get an email after each call the caller spoke on, phone and website calls alike, test calls included. It's off until you turn it on: under **Communication**, tick **Email** on the **Call summary** row. It goes by email only.

Owners and admins choose for the business under **Emails after each call**:

- **Who can get them**: **Owners and admins** (the default), **Everyone on the team**, or **Nobody**. People left out stop getting them from the next call.
- **What they hold**: **Summary and a link** (the default), or **Summary and the whole conversation**, which names the row **Call transcript**. Card and ID numbers are taken out of the email. Copies stay in people's mailboxes after the dashboard deletes the transcript, even when they leave the team.

Replies to the email go to the business owner.

## Apply the settings

LobbyStack saves each channel or event change when you make it. Confirm that the account email and verified phone number belong to someone responsible for the workspace.
