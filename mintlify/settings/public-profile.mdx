The **Settings** > **Public profile** page publishes a page for your business on the same web address as your dashboard, such as `https://app.example.com/profile/acme-dental`. Share the link anywhere you'd list your business: social profiles, email signatures, or a [QR code](/settings/qr-code) at your front desk.

Owners and admins can change and publish the page. Other members can see its settings and preview it.

## Publish the page

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Public profile settings">
    Go to **Settings** > **Public profile**. The page's address is at the top.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your details">
    Enter a **Category**, such as `Dental clinic`, a **Description**, a **Public email**, and your **Address**. Your business name and phone number come from the rest of your settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what the page shows">
    Under **What the page shows**, turn **Phone number**, **Opening hours**, **Services**, and **Map** on or off. A section with nothing to show stays hidden.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the chat">
    Under **Chat**, choose one of your active website widgets, or **No chat**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Turn on **Published**, then click **Save**. Click **Open page** to see it as visitors do.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What visitors see

- Your business name, category, city, and description.
- Whether you're open now, and when you open or close next.
- The chat with your AI receptionist, where visitors can also start a call from their browser. Without a website widget, the page shows your phone number in its place.
- The next free appointment time, when your AI receptionist books appointments itself.
- Your opening hours for the week, in your business's time zone, and closures in the next 30 days.
- Your services, with how long each one takes.
- Your address on a map, with directions, and your phone number, email, and website.
- A note that an AI receptionist answers your calls and chats.

The page shows in English, French, Spanish, or Serbian, following the visitor's browser. Visitors can switch languages on the page.

Search engines can list a published page. Opening hours and services edited in the dashboard show on the page right away.

<Tip>
  The chat on the page uses the widget's own settings: its colors, greeting, and lead form. You don't need to add the page's address to the widget's allowed websites.
</Tip>

## Preview before you publish

Click **Preview** in **Settings** > **Public profile** while the page isn't published. Members of your business who are signed in see the page with a banner saying only your team can see it. The chat shows once the page is published.

Visitors who aren't members get a page that says it doesn't exist.

## Unpublish the page

Turn off **Published** and click **Save**. The page stops loading for visitors right away, and its chat stops answering there. Your settings are kept for when you publish again.

## Change the page's address

The address ends with your business's slug, the short name LobbyStack gave your business when you created it, such as `acme-dental`. You can't change the slug yet.
