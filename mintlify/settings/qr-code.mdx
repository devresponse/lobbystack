The **Settings** > **QR code** page makes printable signs with a QR code that opens your [public profile](/settings/public-profile). Put them on your door, your counter, or your tables. The code is made in your browser from the page's address, and nothing is saved.

Every member of your business can print the signs.

## Print a sign

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open QR code settings">
    Go to **Settings** > **QR code**. **Opens** shows the address the code opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a template">
    **Signal** is a yellow sign. **Front desk** is white and uses less toner. **Night** is black, for dim rooms.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the message">
    Type the sign's headline in **Message**, up to 80 characters. Press Enter to start a new line. The text shrinks to fit the size you choose.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a size">
    Every size prints on A4 paper: one **Full page** sign, two **Half page** (A5) signs, four **Quarter page** (A6) signs, or eight **Cards** (A7), with dashed lines to cut along. Each size shows how wide its code prints and how far away it scans.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what's inside the code">
    Under **Inside the code**, choose **Name** to put the LobbyStack name in the middle of the code, **Logo** for its logo, or **Nothing**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the sign's language">
    Under **Language**, choose the language of the words printed on the sign. It can differ from your dashboard's language.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Print">
    Click **Print**. In the print window, choose A4 paper and print at actual size (100%), not **Fit to page**. Each sign keeps at least 5 mm of blank paper around it, so a laser printer's margins don't cut it off.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Scan the printed sign with two phones before you put it up. Matte paper scans better than glossy paper. For a PDF, choose **Save as PDF** in the print window.
</Tip>

## What the sign says

- Your business name.
- Your message, as the headline.
- "Scan to chat with us" when your public profile has a chat, or "Scan to visit our page" when it doesn't.
- The page's address, for anyone who'd rather type it.
- "Answered by LobbyStack".

## Before your profile is published

Until your public profile is published, anyone who scans the code gets a page that says it doesn't exist. The **QR code** page says so, and links owners and admins to **Public profile** to publish it. You can print the signs first: the code opens the page as soon as it's published, because the address stays the same.

## Download the code alone

Click **Code as PNG** or **Code as SVG** to download just the code, for menus, flyers, or your own designs. The PNG is big enough to print, and the SVG prints sharp at any size.
