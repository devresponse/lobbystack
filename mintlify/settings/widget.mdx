The **Settings** > **Widget** page creates and manages keys for the receptionist widget. Visitors can chat, leave contact details, and start a browser call from the widget.

<Note>
  Website chat is restricted and isn't part of the hosted plans. New widget keys are off by default. On a self-hosted deployment, set `WIDGET_KEY_ISSUANCE_ENABLED=true` to create them. Existing keys keep working.
</Note>

## Configure the widget

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Widget settings">
    Go to **Settings** > **Widget**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure its appearance">
    Choose the color, position, title, subtitle, greeting, and language behavior.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure lead capture">
    Enable the lead form, choose required contact fields, and decide whether it appears before chat.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set allowed websites">
    Enter each exact public origin that can embed the widget, such as `https://www.example.com`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the installation snippet">
    Copy the generated loader snippet and add it before the closing `</body>` tag on your website.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Protect web calls

LobbyStack signs widget sessions and checks the visitor's parent origin against the widget key's allowed origins before it starts a call.

Use an HTTPS website in production. Keep `WIDGET_SESSION_SECRET` and other signing credentials on the server.

## When a visitor asks for a person

Website chats and browser calls can't be transferred to a phone call. When a visitor asks for a person, or one of your rules says to transfer them, the AI receptionist never says it's connecting them.

### Live chat with your team

Turn on **Let visitors chat with your team** on a widget key, and someone from your team can join the chat itself. It's off until you turn it on.

During your opening hours, or at any time if you haven't set any, a visitor who asks for a person in the chat waits for your team:

1. The receptionist tells the visitor it let the team know, and stops answering.
2. Your team gets a **Visitor asking for a person** alert, and the chat shows **Visitor waiting** in [Messages](/dashboard/messages).
3. The first person who replies or clicks **Join chat** has the chat. The visitor sees the first name on their profile.
4. If nobody joins within 3 minutes, the receptionist takes the chat back and asks the visitor for their name and number, so someone can call them back.

Turn the alert on or off for email and SMS under **Settings** > **Widget**.

### Callbacks

Outside opening hours, on browser calls, and when live chat is off, the receptionist offers to take a message so your team can call the visitor back.

When you've set a **Default transfer number**, the receptionist also gives the visitor your business phone number, so they can call and ask for a person there. It follows your transfer rule: if transfers are only for urgent issues, for example, it gives the number for urgent matters.

Callback requests appear in the dashboard like other messages.

## Manage widget keys

Each key has its own origin allowlist and configuration. You can edit an active key, pause it temporarily, or revoke it permanently. Revoking a key stops every embed that uses it.

## Verify the installation

Open the public website in a private browser window. Confirm that the widget loads, starts a chat, and displays the contact form. If web calls are enabled, grant microphone access and place a test call.
